QQ Intro Tuner

The entrance of the hero when the home page opens: the block at the photo's foot rises, then the title line by line, the countdown row by row and the links one by one. Change a number or a curve and press Play (or the page plays it again by itself).

A curve: x is time, y the way travelled. Drag its two handles, type its four numbers, or pick a preset. «Lift» is how far a part rises from; «stagger» how much later the next part of the group starts.
