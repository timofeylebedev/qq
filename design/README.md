# The system

How the QQ site is put together: one range of sizes, one ladder, three layout steps, a few colours, one grammar.
Everything here is checked by `npm run rules` (build/rules.mjs) and generated by `npm run tokens` (build/tokens.mjs).

## Sizes: one range, height included

Every size of the site is two numbers in `design/scale.json`: its value on a 320px screen and on a 1440px screen. In
between it is a straight line; outside it is constant. In CSS each is one `clamp()` written by the generator:

```
clamp(least, value at 320 + (screen − 320) × change / 1120, most)
```

**The screen counts as only as big as its height allows.** A screen 360px tall gets the sizes of a 320 screen, one
920px tall the sizes of a 1440 screen, a straight line between: `--vp = min(100vw, 2 × screen height − 400px)`. So a
phone turned sideways (844 × 390 → counts as 380) gets the phone's sizes, and a 1366 × 650 laptop window the sizes of
a 900px screen — all of them together, so their proportions hold.

**Above 1440 nothing grows.** The content column stops at 1520px (as in the Figma frames of 1920 and 2560) and the
margins grow. Type is written in rem, so a visitor's bigger browser text makes everything bigger.

**One ladder for every size:** 0, 2 … 24 (steps of 2), 28 … 64 (4), 72 … 128 (8), 144 … 256 (16). Both ends of every
role sit on it (sizes of blocks, marked `size`, excepted). Figma's values were the starting point and were moved to the
ladder.

### Type

Two voices: **mono** (JetBrains Mono Bold, in capitals, tracked 0.05em) for names and headings — it stands in for PT
Mono Bold, which Google Fonts has only in Regular (decision `font-headings`); **sans** (Apercu Pro) for everything you
read. The countdown is Technology Bold (made tabular by `build/fonts.py`), sized so it is exactly as wide as its caption
«До ближайшей обжарки» (decision `countdown-as-caption`); a date in its place is set in the mono.

| Role | 320 → 1440 | Family | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `type/display` | 40 → 64 | mono | the hero title «Кофе свежей обжарки» |
| `type/h2` | 32 → 48 | mono | section headings, the catalog tabs, the product's name on its page |
| `type/h3` | 28 → 40 | mono | headings of the image blocks (approach, about) |
| `type/title` | 22 → 32 | mono | the name of the featured coffee |
| `type/card-title` | 18 → 24 | mono | names on product cards, titles of tiles (business, info blocks, product facts), footer headings |
| `type/label` | 14 → 20 | mono (label) | eyebrows above headings («Наш подход»): set apart by colour and case, not size |
| `type/statement` | 24 → 48 | sans | the big sentence of a product's story |
| `type/lead` | 18 → 24 | sans | the caption over the featured photo, the text under a section heading |
| `type/body-l` | 16 → 20 | sans | longer text: the featured description, the story columns, business text |
| `type/body` | 14 → 16 | sans | card descriptions, lists, links of the navbar and the footer, fields |
| `type/small` | 12 → 14 | sans | descriptor pills, captions above values in the buy bar, the copyright |
| `type/control` | 14 → 20 | sans (label) | big actions and values set in bold capitals: hero links, «В корзину», buy-bar values |
| `type/price` | 16 → 18 | sans (label) | prices and weights on cards (medium capitals) |
**The levels keep their distance.** Each pair in `type.relations` is at least ×1.125 apart at both ends of the range,
and so at every size in between (the ratio of two straight lines can only move from its value at one end to its value at
the other): display/h2, h2/h3, h3/title, title/card-title, statement/lead, lead/body-l, body-l/body, body/small and
card-title/body-l. Labels (eyebrows, control text, prices) are set apart by case and weight, not size.

## Layout: one column, three steps

The content column is `width: min(100% − 2 × gutter, 1520px)`, centred, and a container named `col`. Every block
changes its layout when the column reaches one of three shared widths:

| Step | Column | Viewport (about) | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | 22rem (352px) | 390 | the featured coffee's facts side by side; the footer's links in two columns |
| M | 43rem (688px) | 768 (with room for a classic scrollbar) | two columns of cards; image blocks side by side; three info blocks; the hero title over two cells |
| L | 58rem (928px) | 1024 | three columns of cards (the catalog: halves, then thirds); the featured coffee as two squares; the hero in a row of three; the product's fact tiles scattered; the buy bar in one row; the footer in two halves |

Full-width blocks (hero, image blocks, info blocks, footer, buy bar) put their content on the column too, so they
change together with everything else and every block's text starts at the same edge. The only page-level steps:
the navbar's four steps (`media.css`): every section from 64em (the screen of L), four short ones from 48em (the screen
of M), the burger below, and under 40em a tab bar at the foot of the screen with the catalog, bag, favourites and
profile. One more container step, `max` (1520px), is the column at its most: the hero frames its cells there.

## Colour

One ink, cool grey `#2d2f33`, in alpha steps on light grounds; white in alpha steps on dark ones. Grounds: white page,
cloud `#f6f6f6` for cards and tiles, cool grey for the business block, black for the hero and photos. Lavender
`#b190ec` (on dark) / `#7f4fc9` (on light) is the brand's one colour: eyebrows and icons, never a surface.
Descriptor dots have a palette by flavour family (chocolate, nuts, citrus, berry, orchard, sweet, tea, tropical, vine,
spice). Fourteen text/ground pairs are checked for contrast at every build; secondary text is ink-70 (the Figma greys
ink-60 and ink-50 fail for small text: decision `secondary-text`).

## Grammar

- **Cells and hairlines.** Controls and groups are cells divided by 1px lines (the hero's cells, the buy row of a card,
  the filters, the arrows, the info blocks, the business band, the footer); lists of things are cards with gaps.
  Every line is `--line`: cool grey at 20% on light grounds, white at 30% on dark ones (each section says its ground,
  `data-ground`; `build/rules.mjs` checks). Only a state (the current pager dot, a focused field) draws in ink.
- **Grounds lead.** The navbar and the tab bar take the colours of the section under them once they stand wholly on it.
- **Hover tints one thing.** Behind the item under the pointer, white at 20% on dark grounds, ink at 6% on light ones;
  nothing else on the bar dims.
- **No rounded corners.** Only pills and dots are round.
- **A card is one step off its ground**: cloud cards on white, black photo cards, black business cards on cool grey.
- **Names are mono capitals**, everything you read is Apercu.
- **Motion:** things arrive on `--ease-out`, travel on `--ease-move`, leave on `--ease-leave`; hovers come in at 0.2s and
  go out at 0.45s. The 3D packs lean with the page as it scrolls (seen from above or below, like goods on a shelf) and
  turn toward the pointer; they trail their place a little while the page scrolls and catch up as it stops; pressed,
  a pack moves away from the viewer and stays while held. On a coffee's page the pack turns once around as its facts
  pass. The hero's cells rise into place when the page opens (`--t-rise`). With «reduce motion» nothing moves.

## The 3D pack

One WebGL canvas per page draws every pack into its slot (`src/scripts/pouch3d.js`, about 10 KB, no libraries), so 25
packs cost one context. A still of the same pack shows until the canvas has drawn, and for good without WebGL. Every
pack shows the Sumatra label for now (decision `pack-label`). How the model is built: `docs/3d-pack-notes.md`.

## Decisions

`design/decisions.json` lists every place the site departs from a rule on purpose (exceptions), every deliberate
choice the rules don't make by themselves (decisions), and every stand-in (placeholders), each with who decided, when,
why and what would replace it.
