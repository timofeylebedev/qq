## Misfits and questionable results, by cause

### Quiz rule: the taste reason is added even when the coffee lacks most of that taste (29, 35, 37: misfits)
- These three get the same result: method espresso, moka or turka, milk «yes», now «shop», taste «caramel», and Q-Punch first. The first card's
  `why` says «карамель и фрукты, как вы хотели». It fires because Q-Punch is tagged `caramel` (from «песочное печенье, сладкие специи»). The same card's
  `taste` says «Почти без кислинки» with no fruit, and its `but` says «Но мягче, чем вы описали». Someone who picked «Карамель и фрукты (с лёгкой кислинкой)» reads a reason and its denial in one card.
- Cause behind it: the «пачка из супермаркета» anchor (bright 1, body 4.5, and only Q-Optimistic and Q-Punch) plus the milk shift pull «caramel» down to
  the soft-dense corner. The second coffee, Q-Profit (acidity 3, fruit, milk-tagged), fits the stated taste better.
- For 29 the robusta in Q-Punch also goes against «Арабика, т.к. сбалансированный».
- Side finding: Q-Punch is also tagged `wild`, which looks like a data slip.

### Quiz rule: the «special» anchor assumes a filter brewer (39: questionable)
- `now: special` anchors to «фильтр, воронка» (body 2.78). A turka brewer who said they brew in a turka because it is «самый насыщенный» gets Перу Пальма Реаль
  (density 2) and the summary «мягкого и лёгкого». With a turka, moka or espresso method, the special anchor should be the espresso one, or at least not pull body below 3.

### Coding ambiguity, made worse by the milk rule (30: questionable)
- Respondent 30 ticked «Молоко» but wants bright, acidic black filter. With milk «yes», `bright -0.4` plus the 0.45 untagged cost removes every bright coffee, yet
  the first card still says «ягоды и цитрусы, как вы хотели». With milk «some» they would get Гицумби first. The quiz's own wording («Обычно с молоком»
  vs «Когда как») would likely lead this person to «Когда как», so the real-world risk is moderate.

### Small wording issues inside «fits»
- 32: the summary says «Начните со сбалансированного кофе: … хотите необычного». The region word «сбалансированного» clashes with the request for something unusual.
- 46: `now: instant` gets the reason «похож на кофе из магазина», a shop wording for someone who said instant or capsules.

## Suggestions for the quiz's rules and words
1. **Add the taste-match reason only when the coffee is close to the option's acidity as well as sharing a flavour tag.** For example, skip `tasteMatch`
   when the coffee's own `but` is on the `bright` axis. Never show «как вы хотели» and «Но мягче, чем вы описали» on one card.
2. **Keep the stated taste in charge of the first coffee.** With «caramel» or «bright», do not let the shop anchor plus milk push the first coffee to acidity 1. One option is to let the
   taste's `bright` target set a floor, for example at least 2.5 for caramel, or to raise the taste weight above the anchor when now = shop.
3. **Make the «special» anchor depend on the method:** «эспрессо» or «капучино, латте» for espresso, moka and turka, and «фильтр, воронка» only for filter methods.
4. **Milk with a bright request:** when milk is «yes» and taste is «bright», keep at least one coffee with acidity 4 or more among the three (as a «Ярче» move, flagged
   «QQ не советует его для молока»), so a person who asked for brightness sees some.
5. **Summary words:** in «wild» mode say «Начните с необычного кофе» instead of the region adjective, and for «instant» use a reason of its own
   (for example «мягче и плотнее растворимого, только из зерна») instead of the shop sentence.
6. **Data check:** review Q-Punch's `tastes` (`caramel`, `citrus`, `wild`). «Песочное печенье, сладкие специи, цедра» is not wild and arguably not caramel.
