## Misfits and questionable results by cause

### Quiz rule
- **75 (misfit): the taste reason is kept even when `shop` overrides the taste.** With now=`shop`, the first pick is a dense blend (Q-Punch) whatever the taste answer. Its `why` still says "карамель и фрукты, как вы хотели", and its `but` says "мягче, чем вы описали". The card contradicts itself, and the person who said they like fruit/floral/berry gets shortbread and spice.
- **62, 72: third picks that are not for milk go to milk drinkers.** For milk=`yes`, the "ярче"/"необычнее" slot often takes a coffee QQ does not tag for milk (Гицумби, Монте Верде). The `but` is honest, but a milk drinker gets one coffee in three that QQ itself would not recommend for them (also seen in 59, 66, 74, 79, which still count as fits). In 62 that slot is also a Peru, which the respondent dislikes. The quiz cannot know that.
- **72: the spread ignores method and milk.** "Не знаю" with moka + milk gives three medium-bodied coffees, one of them not for milk. With now=`shop` the same spread is all milk-friendly and includes a dense blend, so the rule already exists but is applied only on the `shop` path.
- **76: wild picks drift to calmer coffees.** The wild route picks a medium-acid fermented coffee (Ранчо Гранде) and then "классичнее" and "мягче". For someone who wants "необычные, яркие" coffees, two of the three move away from what they asked for. The summary also says "сбалансированного кофе" for a "необычное" choice (same in 69).

### Coding ambiguity, made worse by a rule
- **70, 78: Tasty drinkers coded `special`.** By the brief's rule, naming Tasty Coffee means `special`, and the quiz then serves softer, lighter coffees ("мягкого и лёгкого"). These two want dark/"насыщенный" coffee (auto machine with milk and sugar, and moka "крепость"). They would probably tick "из магазина" in the real quiz and get Q-Optimistic, which fits. The rule part: on `special` + `choc`, the quiz drops body even for moka and automatic machines.

## Suggestions for the quiz's rules and words
1. **Make `why` agree with the pick.** When `shop` (or milk) puts a coffee first that does not match the chosen taste, drop "…, как вы хотели" and keep only the honest reasons ("похож на кофе из магазина", "QQ советует для капучино"). Never show a taste-match `why` and "мягче, чем вы описали" on the same card.
2. **For milk=`yes`, fill the second and third slots with milk-tagged coffees first** (Бекеле, Q-Finch, Q-Berry, Санта Мария, Луна, Куа Мон are available). Fall back to a coffee not tagged for milk only if none fits.
3. **Let the method set a minimum body.** For moka, turka and automatic machines, with taste `choc` or `dunno`, keep at least one dense coffee (density ≥ 4) in the three, whatever the `now` answer. Do not describe the first pick as "лёгкий" to a moka brewer.
4. **Wild route:** after the first fermented coffee, offer another fermented coffee in a different direction (bright: Ла Сирена / ИПА; dense: Воносалам / Кисии) rather than "классичнее" and "мягче". Use "необычного" rather than "сбалансированного" in the summary.
5. **Option wording for `now`:** "Спешелти других обжарщиков (светлая обжарка, фильтр, моносорта)" is clear for enthusiasts. People who buy Tasty or Yellow Submarine blends for milk fall between it and "Из магазина". Consider "Из магазина или маркетплейса (пачка зерна или молотого)" so they land on the dense path.
6. **Grind defaults:** grinder owners on espresso (60, 69, 74 own DF64 / Eureka) get "помол для эспрессо" by default. Pre-ground espresso goes stale fastest, so default espresso to beans, or ask "есть кофемолка?". Moka defaults to beans while turka and pour-over default to ground. Make this consistent, or make it visible that it can be changed.
7. **Bright route variety:** all ten `bright` filter respondents got exactly the same three (Гицумби, Антиокия, Вуш-Вуш). That is fine for newcomers, but QQ might rotate in Найваша or Ла Сирена for people who already drink bright filter.
