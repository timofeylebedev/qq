## Misfits and questionable, by cause

### Quiz rules
- **Decaf is never shown** (`eligible` drops every decaf: "nobody asked for one"). This affects #17 (misfit: decaf is their only liked coffee) and #14 (questionable: liked "Бразилия декаф").
- **Reasons contradict each other or overclaim the taste.** In #5, Q-Punch gets "карамель и фрукты, как вы хотели" and also "Но мягче, чем вы описали". Milk + shop pulls the first pick to an acidity-1 blend, yet the taste reason is still claimed. The same overclaim shows up in fits: Q-Berry and Куа Мон are called "карамель и фрукты, как вы хотели" with no caramel in their notes.
- **Milk drinkers with now=special get a non-milk third pick and no dense blend.** In #22 (one-button machine, milk + sugar) and #25 (milk, dark roast), the third pick is Гицумби with "QQ не советует его для молока". The `but` is honest, but a milk drinker is handed one coffee in three that QQ itself says is wrong for them.
- **No way to say "dark roast".** #25 asked for "Темная" and got light filter coffees.
- Lesser point: a fermented "Необычнее" third (Монте Верде) for a "с недоверием к новому" respondent (#14).

### Coding ambiguity
- #15: a "кофемашина" with ground coffee and no grinder is probably a рожковая or capsule-type machine, not an automatic one. Coded auto, the quiz sends beans. A real respondent would pick their machine type, so this is mainly a coding risk.
- #22: "tastycoffe" read as specialty. Coded shop, the result would have been dense milk blends, which fit better.
- #25: the taste code (caramel) is a compromise between "Темная" roast and fruity/berry notes.

## Suggestions
1. **Decaf**: add a way to ask for decaf (a fifth option on the taste step, or a "без кофеина" toggle). At least mention Колумбия Декаф in the catalogue line when milk = yes or for moka and machine users.
2. **Taste reasons**: say "как вы хотели" only when the coffee's notes actually contain the chosen family. Otherwise use "ближе всего к тому, что вы выбрали". Never pair it with a `but` that says the opposite (#5).
3. **Milk = yes**: don't fill the "Ярче" slot with a coffee QQ does not tag for milk. Use the next milk-tagged one (e.g. Q-Profit or Q-Berry), or a dense blend when the method is an espresso method.
4. **Bright result variety**: all 9 bright filter brewers got the same three (Гицумби, Антиокия, Вуш-Вуш). Five of them named Кения, and Кения Найваша ПБ never appears. Consider rotating it in as "Ярче" or as the first pick.
5. **Grind**: the set text is fixed per method. Pour-over and drip always say "помол для фильтра", even for owners of a Comandante, Timemore or Chestnut (#2, #3, #8, #16, #23, #24), who want beans. Moka always says "в зёрнах", but moka users often have no grinder. Say both are possible ("в зёрнах или молотый — выберете в корзине").
6. Consider "тёмная обжарка" wording on the choc option ("Шоколад и орехи, без кислинки, как тёмная обжарка"), so dark-roast people (#25) recognise themselves.
