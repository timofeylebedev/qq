## Misfits and questionable ones, by cause

### Quiz rules
- **French press → beans only (102, misfit).** For press the quiz offers only «в зёрнах — крупного помола у QQ пока нет», even to someone without a grinder. Coded `turka` instead, she would get «помол для турки», which works for steeping in a mug. Coded `none`, she gets beans again.
- **«Воронка или дрип-пакет» puts two kinds of people in one answer (93, 94; 95 to a lesser degree).** Drip-bag users often have no funnel and no grinder (94: «Нет» grinder). They get 250 g of «помол для фильтра» as if they owned a V60.
- **Wild + milk gives three «не для молока» warnings (93).** None of the unusual coffees are milk-tagged, so every card repeats the same warning. Куа Мон (milk-tagged, ferment 0.5) could fill one slot.
- **A shop drinker who wants caramel/fruit and brews filter (94)** starts on Мерури (грейпфрут, табачный лист), with acidity-4 Чайна Альта as the alternative. That is a big jump from a supermarket pack, and the softest move (Бекеле) is not first.
- **Robusta is not mentioned (102).** The choc/shop/instant region falls on Q-Optimistic and Q-Punch, which both contain robusta, and the taste line doesn't say so.

### Coding ambiguity (did not change the verdict)
- 102: capsules at home aren't a quiz option. `press` was a guess, and `turka` or `none` were also possible. Every option leads to robusta blends, and `press`/`none` also lead to beans only.
- 87: turka (used most) or pourover (preferred for taste): both give Гицумби / Антиокия or Q-Berry / Вуш-Вуш, and both fit.
- 100: choc or wild: both fit.
- 101: caramel or choc: both fit (choc returns her favourite Q-Optimistic).
- 95: milk `some` (by the rule) or `no` makes no difference here: no pick is milk-tagged in either case.
- 84, 89, 94, 97, 98: `now` was a guess because they name no other roaster. 94 as `special` would get Куа Мон / Уганда / Гицумби, a softer start than with `shop`.

## Other observations (not counted as misfits)
- **Little variety.** 25 people produced 10 distinct trios using 16 of the 24 non-decaf coffees. Caramel + espresso-roast methods → Q-Berry / Бекеле / (Куа Мон or Гицумби) for 7 people. Wild → Ранчо Гранде / Ла Сирена / Кисии for 5. Bright with a filter roast → Гицумби / Антиокия / Вуш-Вуш for 4. Caramel + filter → Куа Мон / Уганда / Гицумби for 3. The picks fit, but a third of the catalogue (e.g. Эль Пласэр ИПА, Кения Найваша, Перу Пальма Реаль, Руанда Ньяки) never appears.
- **The roast changes within a turka set (87, 104):** espresso roast for Гицумби and Антиокия, «Обжарка под фильтр, помол для турки» for Вуш-Вуш, with no word on why.
- **Q-Punch as «Тоже подходит» after Q-Optimistic (88, 102)** is the same position on the taste map, so it is not really a third option.
- **«Эль Пласэр ИПА» never appears for wild,** though respondent 106 named the hop lot as the one he liked.

## Suggestions
1. **Press:** offer the turka/espresso grind with a note («крупного помола нет — подойдёт помол для турки, настаивайте дольше»), or ask «есть кофемолка?» before giving beans only.
2. **Split «Воронка или дрип-пакет»,** or add a follow-up for drip-bag users: suggest drip bags if QQ sells them, otherwise say plainly that ground filter coffee needs a funnel.
3. **Wild + milk:** keep one milk-tagged slot (Куа Мон) and say «не для молока» once in the summary instead of on all three cards.
4. **Mention robusta** in the taste line of blends that contain it («с робустой — крепче и плотнее»), so people who dislike robusta can skip them.
5. **Shop + filter + caramel:** start on the softest move (Бекеле or Уганда Луна) and keep Чайна Альта out of the first result for supermarket drinkers.
6. **Replace a near-duplicate «Тоже подходит»** (Q-Punch after Q-Optimistic) with a real step, e.g. Бразилия Санта Мария.
7. **Turka sets:** keep one roast within a result, or add «эту обжарку под фильтр тоже можно в турку».
