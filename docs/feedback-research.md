# Post-delivery taste feedback: evidence for QQ Coffee

Researched 2026-11-26. "Unverified" = I looked and could not find a primary source.

## 1. Where, when and what they ask

**Coffee**
- **Trade:** you rate in your account under Past Deliveries or My Ratings. The help page doesn't give the scale. Ratings feed the algorithm and support agents, and support staff can build a personal recommendation from your feedback and how you brew. https://trade-coffee.gorgias.help/en-US/how-do-i-rate-m-324468 , https://trade-coffee.gorgias.help/en-US/what-if-i-dont-324487
  - A reviewer got an email inviting a rating shortly after the first bag arrived. https://www.inverse.com/input/reviews/trade-coffee-subscription-review
  - Another describes thumbs up/down, where thumbs-down steers away from similar coffees. https://theadultman.com/live-and-learn/trade-coffee-review/
  - Homegrounds says you rate after "a few cups". https://www.homegrounds.co/trade-coffee-review/
  - Unverified: the exact email delay, any follow-up reason questions, and whether there is an app push.
- **Driftaway:** in your account, mark each coffee in the tasting kit with a heart or a broken heart, ideally after each brew. A detailed mode adds body and acidity ratings and notes. Driftaway says it adjusts later shipments from these notes. https://driftaway.coffee/complete-guide-tasting-kit/?amp
- **MistoBox:** 5 stars plus notes on the Your Collection page. You choose whether your curator sees it. https://mistobox.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000125334
- **Crema:** after delivery, under Orders & Ratings. Ratings feed a "Brew Log" and its recommendations. https://intercom.help/crema-coffee/en/articles/4461787-how-do-i-rate-my-coffees
  - Crema's workplace product uses four labels: Excellent / Good / Dull / Not sure. "Good" means you liked it but don't want it again soon, so even this short scale separates liking from wanting a repeat. https://intercom.help/crema-coffee/en/articles/3355309-how-do-i-rate-the-coffees-i-try
- **Pact (UK):** a "Rate Coffees" page in the account that updates your taste profile. https://support.pactcoffee.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406002896658-Can-I-rate-my-coffees
- **Cometeer:** rate coffees in Order History or the app. It says more ratings make boxes "smarter". https://cometeer.com/blogs/news/cometeer-coffee-profile-feature
- **Bean Box:** only a heart for favourites. Bags carry a QR code, which a reviewer used to reorder, not to rate. https://www.mysubscriptionaddiction.com/bean-box-review
- **Onyx:** Roaster's Choice takes no steering, so there is no feedback loop. https://onyxcoffeelab.com/pages/subscription-faqs
- **Atlas:** the help page covers only changing roast preference. https://help.atlascoffeeclub.com/en-US/can-i-adjust-my-roast-type-519516
- **Unverified:** Atlas ratings, Blue Bottle, Coffee Circle, Tasty Coffee and other Russian roasters. I found no public post-delivery taste loop for any of them.

**Other taste-fit retailers**
- **Stitch Fix:** feedback is part of checkout, the step where you must say what you keep or return. Each item gets tap options for size, fit, style, quality and price, plus optional comments, then a question about the whole Fix. https://support.stitchfix.com/support/solutions/articles/153000250414-how-to-leave-feedback-on-your-fix-items
  - Liked/disliked attributes are pulled out of the free text, and items with disliked attributes are shown less. https://multithreaded.stitchfix.com/blog/2022/07/19/your-opinion-matters/
- **Naked Wines:** three buttons (Loved it / Liked it / Not for you) replaced "would buy again". Each says what it does next: find similar, recommend more, or avoid similar. https://nakedwines.co.uk/ratings
- **Winc:** 5 stars, each with a meaning. https://wincwine.gorgias.help/en-US/how-do-i-rate-and-add-notes-for-a-wine-i%E2%80%99ve-received-298854
- **Vivino:** personal match scores start after 5 ratings. https://www.vivino.com/wine-news/introducing-match-for-you
- **Hungryroot:** thumbs up/down in a carousel on the homepage and the Delivered page, once you have cooked. https://blog.hungryroot.com/post/hungryroot-101-rating-your-deliveries
- **HelloFresh:** reviews ratings weekly and changes recipes based on them. https://blog.hellofresh.com.au/?p=202581

**Pattern:** a rating lives in the account next to the order, an email points to it, and the answer is usually 2–4 named options rather than a long form.

## 2. Timing

**Coffee rest.** There is no single right day.
- Partners Coffee: rest filter at least 5–7 days and espresso 7–10 days after roasting, with flavour best at roughly 5–12 days. https://www.partnerscoffee.com/blogs/education/a-guide-to-resting-coffee
- Scott Rao: drum-roasted filter coffee needs about 1–2 days. Light air-roasted coffee can need 1–4 weeks. https://www.scottrao.com/blog/restingbeans
- So the ask should depend on roast date and brew method, not delivery date alone. It should also come after a few brews, because the first cups are often spent dialling in the grind (Homegrounds on Trade's "few cups", above).

**Review requests**
- **Jung et al., Journal of Marketing (two field experiments, over 300,000 consumers):** asking as soon as possible was not best. Reminders sent before customers would normally write a review caused problems. Timing barely changed ratings or sentiment. https://ama.org/2023/01/10/asking-for-customer-reviews-at-the-right-time-sooner-is-not-always-better
- **PowerReviews (vendor guidance):** wait 14 days for perishables, then send one follow-up 7 days later. Among reviewers, 68% wrote after the first request and 28% after the second. https://www.powerreviews.com/when-to-ask-for-reviews-best-practice-guide/
- **Judge.me:** the default is 14 days after fulfilment, and over 76% of its stores keep it. It suggests a reminder after 3–5 days. https://judge.me/blog/review-requests-4-key-points-to-get-more-responses-and-better-reviews
- **Feefo:** for products, response rises with a longer delay, and about 8 days is often best for retail. It shows no figures. https://business.feefo.com/resources/when-to-ask-for-feedback-data-backed-tips-to-boost-engagement

## 3. Response rates

**Answer buttons in the email beat a link.**
- **Liu & Inchausti 2017, Survey Practice:** a randomised test with about 8,700 valid emails. The embedded first question got 32.0% to start versus 26.2% for a link, and 29.1% to complete versus 24.4%. Answers were the same either way. A first-question answer is saved even if the person stops there. https://www.surveypractice.org/article/2791-improving-survey-response-rates-the-effect-of-embedded-questions-in-web-survey-email-invitations
- **Varghese et al. 2018 (about 45,000 ACT students):** with an embedded item, people were 2.31 times as likely to start and 1.33 times as likely to finish. The lift was biggest when the topic was most on their minds (test day). For QQ, that means asking while the bag is being drunk. https://www.surveypractice.org/article/3127-are-embedded-survey-items-the-solution-to-low-web-survey-response-rates-an-investigation-of-the-interaction-between-embedded-survey-items-and-time-of

**Length**
- **SurveyMonkey (100,000 surveys):** drop-off climbs most steeply over the first 15 questions. https://www.surveymonkey.com/curiosity/survey_questions_and_completion_rates/
- **NN/g:** keep surveys short. Split one long survey into several two-question ones, and fit each on a single screen. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/keep-online-surveys-short/
- **Unverified:** I found no study on 1 versus 2 versus 3 taps for taste feedback specifically.

## 4. Showing it listened

**What services show**
- Naked Wines says on each button what the answer will do. https://nakedwines.co.uk/ratings
- Stitch Fix puts a stylist note in every Fix explaining the picks. https://stitchfix.com/men/blog/?p=8077 . I could not verify that the note quotes your past feedback.
- Vivino shows a % match and a taste profile split into Liked / Disliked. https://www.vivino.com/wine-news/your-unique-taste-visualized
- Spotify lists what shapes recommendations, and "not interested" means fewer similar items. https://www.spotify.com/uk/safetyandprivacy/understanding-recommendations
- "Because you listened to…" carousels are common, but no published engagement data exists for them. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.10528.pdf

**Evidence**
- **Pu & Chen 2006 (72 people):** explanation interfaces that group results with a reason raised perceived competence and intention to return. This was self-reported. https://www.comp.hkbu.edu.hk/~lichen/download/fp7925-pu.pdf
- **Tintarev & Masthoff (UMUAI):** explanations serve seven aims: transparency, scrutability, trust, effectiveness, persuasiveness, efficiency and satisfaction. Personalised explanations raised satisfaction but not decision accuracy. https://abdn.elsevierpure.com/files/20864620/USER700.pdf
- **Buell, Kim & Tsay 2017:** letting customers see the work raised perceived value. Reported quality rose 22%. https://ideas.repec.org/a/inm/ormnsc/v63y2017i6p1673-1695.html
- **Kaul et al., Marketing Science 2025 (field RCT, 274 firms):** firms that sought feedback changed those areas more often and earned more. Spend rose most where the changes matched the feedback. https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/pdf/10.1287/mksc.2023.0324

## 5. A card or QR in the pack

- **Sample Coffee (AU):** the QR on each bag shows the brew window for that roast date (adjusted if pre-ground), the coffee's story and brew tips. It is not a rating. https://samplecoffee.com.au/journal/release-notes-2023-aug-sep
- **Cropster Hub Tag:** a QR on cupping labels for yes/no/maybe plus notes, used at events. No usage data. https://www.cropster.com/de/news/artikel/hub-tag-remember-that-coffee-and-everything-about-it/
- **Driftaway:** kit inserts carry a QR for a virtual tasting, not for feedback. Bean Box: QR on the bag for reordering. https://www.mysubscriptionaddiction.com/driftaway-coffee-review
- **QR benchmark (Scanova via Retail Brew):** about 95% of scans come from 5% of codes. Purpose-built pages get over 7 times the scans of plain site redirects. https://www.retailbrew.com/stories/nearly-95-of-qr-code-scans-come-from-just-5-of-codes-what-gets-consumers-to-scan
- **Unverified:** no roaster published results for a taste-feedback card or QR. The Bitly guide has no named cases.

## What this means for QQ

1. **When:** set the ask from roast date plus method plus bag size, not a fixed delay. Filter: about 7–10 days after roast. Espresso: about 10–14 days. Use the later end for light roasts. Send one reminder about 5 days later, then stop.
2. **Expect a delay:** a 200 g bag runs out sooner than a 1 kg bag. For 1 kg, ask once after about 1–2 weeks of drinking rather than at the end.
3. **The letter:** put the first question in the email as 3 buttons: «слишком ярко / в самый раз / хочу ярче». A tap saves the answer and opens the page with that answer already chosen. The evidence is about 20% more starts and completions.
4. **Taps:** one required tap (the direction). Then one optional screen: «плотнее?», «необычнее?», brew method (pre-filled from the order), and the two non-taste buttons «помол не подошёл» and «не как в описании». Keep it under 30 seconds.
5. **Where:** the same buttons sit on the order page in the account, so a reply is never lost if the email is ignored.
6. **Separate non-taste answers:** like Stitch Fix's separate size/fit/price options, these go to service (a grind tip or a check of the description). They should never move the taste vector.
7. **Say what each button does:** like Naked Wines, a hint under each, e.g. «→ в следующий раз предложим ярче».
8. **Show it listened:** the next recommendation, email and pack card say «Вы сказали “хочу плотнее” — поэтому Колумбия, а не Кения». The research supports this for trust, intention to return and satisfaction, not proven repeat purchase.
9. **The pack:** a small card with a QR to a dedicated page for this lot. The page shows this bag's best-from date for each method and the same 3 buttons. Treat it as a test and measure scans, because no roaster has published results.
10. **Russian market:** I found no Russian roaster doing this publicly. Being first is a point of difference, but there are no local benchmarks yet.
