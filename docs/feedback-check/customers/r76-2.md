# r76, round 2: pour-over, light roast, floral/berry, former QQ customer who left

Quiz: method=pourover, milk=no, now=special, taste=wild, same as round 1. She grinds on a Comandante, drinks it black, wants "яркие дескрипторы" and "постоянно пробует" new coffees.

## Step 1
**Offered:** identical to round 1. The lead was «Начните с необычного кофе: вы уже пьёте спешелти и хотите необычного. Это начало, а не приговор…». The cards were Бразилия Ранчо Гранде («Начните с него»), Колумбия Ла Сирена («Ярче») and Кения Кисии («Необычнее»). The run-together card text is still there («необычный, как вы хотели Лёгкая кислинка…»), and so is the puzzling «Необычнее первого: с ферментацией», when the first card already has «заметная ферментация».
**Bought:** Ла Сирена, because strawberry jam plus "ярче" is exactly what she's after.
**Cup:** co-fermented, acidity 4, body 2, ferment 2. On her V60 it's a juicy, tea-like strawberry cup with a detailed producer and process story she'd value.
**Taps:** bright=ok, body=ok, unusual=ok, all honest.
**Site said:** «Колумбия Ла Сирена: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:». "Moved" is now «уточнился» (round 1 said «ярче и легче» after the same "ok"s). The taste line is clear: «Пока это набросок по подбору и 1 ответу после доставки. Ещё 2 ответа — и назовём ваш вкус словами». "Похожий" was Вуш-Вуш: «Как у него: кислинка и плотность, классичнее». That reads in words, not 0–3 numbers, but it is still a step down in unusualness after an "ok". Ранчо Гранде stayed «По вашему вкусу».
**Verdict: fits.** The pick was a hit and the reply listened.

## Step 2
**Bought:** Вуш-Вуш. It's new to her, a rare variety, and барбариски/pear cider is the kind of novelty she buys for.
**Cup:** natural with 3 days of anaerobic fermentation, acidity 4, body 2, sweetness 5, ferment 1. On a pour-over it's sweet, juicy and very clear.
**Taps:** bright=ok, body=ok, unusual=ok.
**Site said:** «Ещё 1 ответ — и назовём ваш вкус словами» (the countdown is consistent now). "Похожий" was Вьетнам Куа Мон: «Кислинка мягче, плотнее и классичнее». That moves on all three rows away from three "ok"s, and the third card is labelled «Похожий» anyway. She'd also distrust "Вьетнам" on sight, since she associates it with robusta and dark supermarket coffee. In place of «По вашему вкусу», the third card was now a second «Снова» for Ла Сирена («Вы сказали о нём «в самый раз»»), which at least matches what she said.
**Verdict: questionable.** The only new coffee on offer goes against all three of her answers, so the one card she'd consider is a repeat.

## Step 3
**Bought:** Ла Сирена again, reluctantly. She refuses the Vietnam (softer and denser, the opposite of what she asked for) and wouldn't rebuy Вуш-Вуш straight away. Of the two coffees she liked, Ла Сирена is the brighter and more experimental one, but she'd rather have had something new.
**Cup:** the same as step 1, the same good cup.
**Taps:** bright=ok, body=ok, unusual=ok.
**Site said:** the taste line was «Яркий и лёгкий, можно и с заметной ферментацией, уже довольно точно». That is accurate now (round 1 said «с лёгкой ферментацией»). But the offer is a closed loop: «Снова» Ла Сирена, «Снова» Вуш-Вуш, and «Похожий» Ранчо Гранде («Как у него: ферментация, кислинка мягче и плотнее»). "Похожий" again means "differs on two rows she approved". Nothing new is bright, light and fermented, and to someone who "постоянно пробует" it reads as "QQ has nothing else for you", which is why she left before.
**Verdict: questionable.** The summary is right, but there's no new coffee that matches it.

## Overall
Did it help? Partly. She found two coffees she'd rebuy (Ла Сирена in the first order, Вуш-Вуш in the second), but after that the loop could only offer repeats or coffees that contradict her answers. By the third order it had stopped helping her discover anything, and discovery is what she buys for. She'd likely drift back to HQ or Сварщица.

Biggest problem: «Похожий» is still filled with whatever is nearest in the catalogue, even when it differs on rows she approved (Куа Мон is softer, denser and more classic). Meanwhile genuinely close options, like round 1's Найваша ПБ (acidity 4.5, body 2), didn't appear.

Fix: after "ok" on every row, «Похожий» should require the same brightness and lightness and the same or higher fermentation, and rank by unseen descriptors (floral, berry). If nothing meets that, say so plainly («Похожих сейчас нет — вот ближайший, он плотнее») rather than labelling a mismatch «Похожий».

## Against round 1
- Better: "moved" is now «уточнился» after "ok" answers, the taste countdown is consistent, the final taste line keeps «заметной ферментацией», and the 0–3 numbers became words.
- Better: Ранчо Гранде no longer holds «По вашему вкусу» on every screen; a second «Снова» of an approved coffee replaced it.
- Worse: the new-coffee slot got worse. Round 1 offered a bright Kenya; round 2 offered a soft, dense Vietnam and then Ранчо Гранде, so the third order became a forced repeat.
- Still wrong: «Похожий» still steps away on rows she approved, and the quiz card text and the «Необычнее первого» label are unchanged.
