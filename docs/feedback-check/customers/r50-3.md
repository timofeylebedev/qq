# r50, round 3: daily espresso drinker at home who buys on a budget and distrusts new coffees

Profile in brief: a portafilter machine with a built-in grinder. He drinks espresso most days, sometimes a flat white or a raf. He wants a medium roast and blends, pays «до 2000₽» per kg and distrusts new coffees. Quiz: espresso / some / shop / dunno.

## Step 1
**Offered:** the same three cards as before. Lead: «Вкус пока не знаем — поэтому три разных кофе…». The cards were Q-Optimistic «Мягкий и плотный», Эфиопия Бекеле «Мягкий» and Q-Finch «Плотный». There are no prices. **Bought:** Q-Optimistic, the familiar, cheap-looking blend with no sourness.

**Cup:** thick, cocoa-like, a slightly bitter espresso, just what he likes. It arrived ground for espresso, though he grinds his own.

**Taps:** first `issue=grind need=beans`, then bright=ok, body=ok, milk=no.

**Site's reply.** The grind answer got its own confirmation: «Передали в QQ: напишем вам и, если нужно, заменим пачку. Вкус по этому ответу не двигаем. Дальше предложим в зёрнах.» Every card now says «обжарка под эспрессо, в зёрнах». After the taste answer: «Q-Optimistic: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:». The cards were «Снова» Q-Optimistic, «Похожий» Q-Punch («Кислинка и плотность те же») and «По вашему вкусу» Q-Finch.

The in-between offer after the grind answer still has the odd lead «Пока нет ответов про вкус…» and only one card.

**Verdict: fits.** He was heard on both grind and taste, and saw it in plain words.

## Step 2
**Bought:** Q-Optimistic again, and it arrived «В зернах». **Cup:** the same coffee, fresher from his own grinder, drunk mostly in flat whites and rafs. **Taps:** bright=ok, body=ok, milk=yes.

**Site's reply:** the same lead and the same three cards. «Ещё 1 ответ — и назовём ваш вкус словами».

**Verdict: fits.** He has a bag to keep buying, in beans.

## Step 3
**Bought:** Q-Punch, «Похожий». «Кислинка и плотность те же» is the reassurance a cautious buyer needs before trying something new.

**Cup.** Robusta 40%, against 20% in Q-Optimistic. Acidity and body are the same on paper, but as his straight espresso it is heavier and more bitter. Both cards said «крепче и с горчинкой», so he had no warning that this one is much more so. In a raf it is fine.

**Taps:** bright=ok, body=less. This is his nearest word for «too heavy and bitter», because there is no bitterness row. Milk=no.

**Site's reply.** «Q-Punch: вы сказали «слишком плотно». Следующий — легче:». The cards:
- «Легче»: Эфиопия Бекеле, «Мягкая кислинка, средней плотности… Для сравнения, Q-Punch: очень плотный»
- «Тоже легче»: Уганда Луна, «Мягкая кислинка… цитрусы»
- «Снова»: Q-Optimistic, «Вы сказали о нём «в самый раз»…»

His favourite is kept, so he simply goes back to it. The two «lighter» cards are both single origins with «кислинка», and he never asked for that. They also sit oddly under «Мягкий и плотный… уже довольно точно».

**Verdict: questionable.** He isn't lost, but the only new options swing to acidic, medium-bodied coffees. There is no step in between, such as a blend with less robusta.

## Overall
He found a coffee to keep buying on the first order (Q-Optimistic), got it in beans from the second order, and kept it in view after a bad third bag. He would stay with QQ, though the price is still not shown.

**Biggest problem:** the «Похожий» label hides the one difference that matters to an espresso drinker, how much robusta and bitterness the blend has. After the miss, «легче» then jumps straight to acidic single origins.

**Fix:** say what actually differs between similar blends, e.g. «Похожий, но с горчинкой сильнее: робусты 40% против 20%». After «слишком плотно» on a low-acid blend, offer the nearest lighter coffee that is still low in acid before any coffee with «кислинка».

## Against round 2
- **Better:** the grind answer gets a plain confirmation («Дальше предложим в зёрнах»), every card shows «в зёрнах», and «Похожий» is back with a useful «Кислинка и плотность те же».
- **Worse:** that same «Похожий» card led him to a harsher blend without warning. The «легче» step again offers only acidic single origins, while the taste line says «мягкий и плотный».
- **Still wrong:** there are no prices, the one-card «Пока нет ответов про вкус» screen after the grind answer remains, and there is no way to say «too bitter».
