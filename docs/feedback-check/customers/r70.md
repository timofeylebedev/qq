# r70: automatic machine, lots of milk and sugar, likes dark roast, "chocolate and berries"

Quiz: method=auto milk=yes now=special taste=choc. She doesn't understand acidity ("Не понимаю какой люблю больше"), wants a "насыщенный" cup and unusual notes, and remembers a cherry-tasting coffee from Dubai. Her favourite was QQ's blend «Сухой закон», which the site no longer sells.

## Step 1: quiz → Q-Optimistic

**Offered.** The lead was "Начните с мягкого и плотного кофе: вы пьёте с молоком, уже пьёте спешелти и выбрали шоколад и орехи." Three cards: "Начните с него" Q-Optimistic, "Ярче" Q-Profit, "Легче" Уганда Луна. All three say "QQ советует его для капучино и латте" and "кофемашина мелет сама", which reassures her.
**Bought:** Q-Optimistic. It is the first card, it's a blend like her beloved Сухой закон, and "очень плотный… крепче" sounds like the rich cup she wants. The robusta "горчинка" doesn't scare her because she adds sugar.

**Cup.** Acidity 1, body 5, cocoa, nuts, robusta. In an auto machine with lots of milk and sugar it makes a strong, cocoa-ish latte that holds up to milk. It's tasty but plain: there is nothing berry-like or "необычное" in it.
**Taps:** Кислинка **Хочу ярче**. She doesn't know acidity, but she reads «ярче» as "more vivid, more fruit", the cherry she remembers. Плотность **В самый раз**, because it was rich enough.

**Site's reply.** "Q-Optimistic: вы сказали «хочу ярче». Следующий — ярче:". Cards: "Ярче" Q-Profit ("Кислинка 3 (Q-Optimistic — 1)… косточковые фрукты… шоколад"), "Тоже ярче" Индонезия Воносалам ("заметная ферментация… лакрица"), and "По вашему вкусу" Уганда Луна. It clearly listened. "Косточковые фрукты" plus "шоколад" in a dense blend is close to the cherry she wants. She wouldn't know what "ферментация" means, and "лакрица" would put her off. The output also has `"moved": "плотнее"`, although she said body was fine. If a customer can see that, it would puzzle her.

**Verdict: fits.** A dense chocolate blend is the right start for a heavy-milk machine drinker, and the brighter follow-up was a sensible step toward her berries.

## Step 2: Q-Profit

**Bought:** Q-Profit. It is the first "Ярче" card, and it offers chocolate and stone fruit while staying "плотный". It's also a blend.

**Cup.** Acidity 3, body 4, sweetness 3.5. The text says "спелые ягоды, сухофрукты и косточковые фрукты, шоколад и орехи… насыщенную, «кофейную» чашку". Milk tames the acidity into fruitiness, and the body still carries the latte. This is probably the closest thing to Сухой закон / Dubai for her.
**Taps:** Кислинка **В самый раз** and Плотность **В самый раз**, because she got the fruit without losing the richness.

**Site's reply.** "Q-Profit: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:". Cards: "Снова" Q-Profit, "Похожий" Бразилия Санта Мария, and "По вашему вкусу" Уганда Луна. The "Снова" card is good, and she would gladly reorder. But both alternatives are lighter. Santa Maria says outright "Отличие: легче (плотность 3 против 4)", and she would not know the word "урбеч". She said the body was exactly right, yet nothing denser or berrier sits beside the reorder. Her answer was ok/ok, but the output has `"moved": "ярче и легче"`.

**Verdict: fits.** The site found her keeper in two orders and offered it again. The alternatives were weak.

## Step 3: Уганда Луна

**Bought:** Уганда Луна. She "постоянно пробую новое". This coffee was on screen at every step, and the Q-Profit card shows it is half of that blend, so she tries it.

**Cup.** Acidity 2, body 3, sweetness 5, with milk chocolate and dried fruit. Through an auto machine under lots of milk, body 3 goes thin and milky. It's sweet, but "менее насыщенное", which is her exact complaint about Tasty Coffee.
**Taps:** Кислинка **В самый раз**, because she had no complaint there. Плотность **Хочу плотнее**, because it got lost in the milk.

**Site's reply.** "Уганда Луна: вы сказали «хочу плотнее». Следующий — плотнее:". Its taste line read "Мягкий и плотный, без ферментации, уже довольно точно." Cards:
- "Плотнее" Q-Berry ("сухофрукты, чай, шоколад, ягоды", body 4). This is her chocolate and berries, and she would buy it at once.
- "Тоже плотнее" Q-Punch (a robusta blend that is close to Q-Optimistic).
- "По вашему вкусу" Индонезия Воносалам: "Яркая кислинка, плотный, необычный: заметная ферментация."

That last card sits directly under a line telling her that her taste is "мягкий… без ферментации". It contradicts the site's own summary, and it makes the label "По вашему вкусу" untrustworthy. Q-Profit, the one she called "в самый раз", has also disappeared from the offer. Again the output's `"moved": "мягче"` doesn't obviously follow from "хочу плотнее".

**Verdict: questionable.** Q-Berry is a strong pick. But step 2 had pushed her toward a lighter coffee that wasn't needed, and the "По вашему вкусу" card goes against what the site just said about her.

## Overall

**The loop works for her.** In two orders it took her from a quiz guess to Q-Profit, a coffee she'd keep buying, and its last offer, Q-Berry, looks like an even better match ("шоколад, ягоды", dense, made for milk). The taps were easy. The leads quote her own answer back ("вы сказали «хочу ярче»"), which shows the site is listening. She still never learns what "кислинка" means for her, which was exactly her problem.

**Biggest problem:** the "По вашему вкусу" card ignores her stated taste. Уганда Луна, a lighter coffee, came back at every step, even after she said the body was right. At the end, a bright, fermented Indonesian coffee was labelled "По вашему вкусу" right under "Мягкий и плотный, без ферментации". A customer who already finds it "сложно подобрать сорт" will stop trusting the labels.

**Fix:** the "По вашему вкусу" card should satisfy the taste line printed above it. With fermentation 0 in her profile, it should exclude any coffee with ферментация ≥ 1 and acidity more than one step above her current coffee. After "в самый раз", show "Снова" plus alternatives of the same or higher body, never only lighter ones. For milk drinkers, drop jargon such as "ферментация" and "урбеч" from the cards, or explain it, for example "ферментация — винный, необычный вкус".
