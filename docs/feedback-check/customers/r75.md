# r75: turka, milk + sugar + syrup, fruity/floral/berry, "постоянно пробую новое", likes Colombians

Quiz: method=turka milk=yes now=shop taste=caramel.

## Step 1: Q-Berry

**Offered.** Lead: «Начните с плотного кофе: вы пьёте с молоком, привыкли к кофе из магазина и выбрали карамель и фрукты.» The three cards were Q-Berry «Начните с него» (berries, chocolate, «плотный»), Эфиопия Бекеле «Мягче» and Вьетнам Куа Мон «Легче». I bought **Q-Berry**. It was the top pick, it had berries, and it was recommended for milk. Bekele's flowers tempted me, but I took the site's first suggestion.
One thing grated: «привыкли к кофе из магазина». I buy in a specialty coffee shop and «неплохо знаю процессы». This line reads as if I drink supermarket coffee.

**Cup.** It is a blend: Brazil 60%, Rwanda 20%, Ethiopia 20%. Acidity 3, body 4, sweetness 4. In the turka it comes out full and chocolatey. With milk and syrup it makes a fine latte-style drink, but the «деликатная кислотность красных и темных ягод» disappears. That is "nice classic coffee", not the fruit I buy specialty for.
Taps: **bright=more** (with milk the berries are gone and I want fruit) and **body=ok** (turka plus milk, the body is right).

**Site said back.** «Q-Berry: вы сказали «хочу ярче». Следующий — ярче:». It offered Руанда Гицумби (4 vs 3), Колумбия Эль Пласэр ИПА (4.5, «заметная ферментация») and Колумбия Вуш-Вуш «По вашему вкусу». The site clearly listened, and it offered two Colombians, which I love. One odd thing: the response also said «moved: ярче и легче», even though I had said the body was fine.

**Verdict: fits.** A safe milk-friendly start, and the reply took «ярче» correctly.

## Step 2: Колумбия Эль Пласэр ИПА

**Bought** the IPA. It is Colombian, an «интересный сорт», and a hop-and-lactic culturing process. That is exactly the kind of thing I try. It is only roasted for filter, and the card said so honestly: «обжарка под фильтр, помол для турки».

**Cup.** It is a Pink Bourbon with 100 h + 100 h of anaerobic fermentation with lactic acid and hops. Acidity 4.5, body 4, sweetness 5, ferment 2. A filter roast in the turka extracts hard, and the result is very sharp. In my usual cup with milk and sugar the mandarin and passionfruit turned sour against the milk, and the hops tasted strange. Black it was exciting, but too sharp for three cups a day.
Taps: **bright=less** (with milk it was too sour), **body=ok**, and **unusual=ok** (I like the funk and know what culturing is; the problem was acidity, not strangeness).

**Site said back.** «Колумбия Эль Пласэр ИПА: вы сказали «слишком ярко». Следующий — мягче:». It offered Бразилия Ранчо Гранде (Кислинка 3 vs 4.5, «заметная ферментация»), Q-Profit and Вьетнам Куа Мон «По вашему вкусу». It listened on acidity.
- It jumped straight back to 3, which is the level I had just called too dull. It did not offer the 4 in the middle. Руанда Гицумби from the previous offer would have been the natural pick.
- Вьетнам makes me wary, because I drink arabica only.
- The response again said «moved: легче и необычнее», but I had answered body=ok and unusual=ok.

**Verdict: questionable.** The site was right to go softer, but it overshot back to the level I had already rejected, and the "moved" line does not match my answers.

## Step 3: Бразилия Ранчо Гранде

**Bought** Ранчо Гранде. It is a «Twist» semi-anaerobic natural from Brazil, «для тех, кто ищет удивление». Q-Profit looked like another Q-Berry.

**Cup.** Acidity 3, body 3, sweetness 4, ferment 2. Grillage, grapefruit and fruit liqueur. In the turka with milk and a bit of syrup, the liqueur-like fruit and nougat sweetness survive the milk, unlike Q-Berry's berries. Here the fruit comes from the process rather than from sharp acidity, and that suits my milk cup.
There is a small mismatch: the scale says body 3, but the text says «Плотное тело». In the turka it felt full enough.
Taps: **bright=ok**, **body=ok**, **unusual=ok**.

**Final offer.** «Бразилия Ранчо Гранде: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:». The cards were Ранчо Гранде «Снова», Кения Кисии «Похожий» («Та же кислотность (3) и плотность (3)… Отличие: необычнее (фермент 3 против 2)») and Перу Монте Верде «По вашему вкусу» (крем-брюле, сушеный апельсин). This is good. I would reorder Ранчо Гранде and try the Peru next, and I might add the Kenya because I like new things.
What puzzles me is the taste summary: «Плотный, с лёгкой ферментацией, уже довольно точно.»
- Two steps earlier the site called this same coffee «заметная ферментация», and now it says «лёгкой».
- It calls me «плотный» when my "just right" coffee is listed as «средней плотности».
- It still says «moved: легче и необычнее» after three ok answers.

So the words it uses to describe me do not match its own cards.

**Verdict: fits.** I got a reorder and two sensible next steps. Only the summary sentence is off.

## Overall

The loop worked. By order 3 this person had a coffee they would keep buying (Ранчо Гранде), plus a credible next try (Перу Монте Верде). Three orders were enough. One of the three, the IPA, was a sour miss with milk.

**Biggest problem.** The site moves in steps that are too big, and it does not remember the earlier answer. «Хочу ярче» at 3 led to an offer of 4.5. «Слишком ярко» at 4.5 sent me straight back to 3, the level I had just rejected. The second answer threw away the first, even though the 4 in the middle (Руанда Гицумби) had already been offered. A smaller inconsistency on the same theme: the taste and "moved" wording («лёгкой ферментацией», «плотный», «легче и необычнее») contradicts both my taps and the site's own cards.

**One fix.** When a new answer reverses the previous one (more → less), offer something between the two coffees first: here, acidity about 4 with Q-Berry's body. Do not jump to the far side. Separately, build the taste sentence from the same labels the cards use (ferment 2 = «заметная ферментация»). For a quiz answer of now=shop, do not say «кофе из магазина» to someone who buys in a specialty shop.
