## Step 2: Колумбия Чайна Альта

**Bought: Chayna Alta.** It is a Colombian with «цветы», grapefruit and rosehip, and the person named Colombia and floral notes. It arrived **in beans**.

**Cup and taps.** The scales match Rwanda (acidity 4, body 3), but sweetness is 4. It is a washed Castillo regional lot, which the roaster itself calls «понятный… кофе на каждый день». In the drip machine it is caramel-forward and tamer than Rwanda's berries. Taps:
- **Кислинка: хочу ярче.** For a Kenya fan it lacks sparkle.
- **Плотность: в самый раз.**

The «Похожий» line was true on the scales, but the cup was flatter, which the scales do not show.

**Site's reply.** «Колумбия Чайна Альта: вы сказали «хочу ярче». Следующий — ярче:». The cards:
- **«Ярче» Кения Найваша ПБ.** This is spot on, and the comparison line is useful: «Для сравнения, Колумбия Чайна Альта: яркая кислинка.»
- **«Тоже ярче» Колумбия Эль Пласэр ИПА:** «плотный, необычный: заметная ферментация… хмель». The person asked only for more brightness and said the body was fine. This card also changes body and fermentation, and «плотный» is exactly what they avoid.
- **«Снова» Rwanda.**

**Verdict: fits.** The first card is right. The second one ignores «плотность — в самый раз».

## Overall

The person found a keeper (Kenya) **by order three**, after one detour. The «хочу ярче» answer went straight to the right coffee. Grind is now handled end to end.

**Biggest problem:** a "move" card that changes more than was asked. «Тоже ярче» offered a dense, hoppy, fermented coffee to someone who wanted only more brightness. A smaller issue: «Похожий» is judged on acidity and body alone, so Чайна Альта counted as "the same" as a sweeter, juicier Rwanda.

**Fix:** a move card should change only what the person asked to change. After «хочу ярче» with «плотность — в самый раз», keep body within ±0.5 and fermentation no higher than the last cup. Also weigh sweetness and flavour family when choosing «Похожий».

## Against round 2

- **Better:** the grind answer is confirmed in words, the cards say «в зёрнах», and the comparison texts are clear again.
- **Worse:** «Тоже ярче» changes body and fermentation along with acidity, and Kenya dropped out of «Похожий» after the first «в самый раз», so it took one order longer to reach it.
- **Still wrong:** the quiz never asks about grinding, so the first bag still arrives ground and needs a complaint to fix.
