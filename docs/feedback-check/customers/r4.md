# Respondent 4: drip machine, black, light roast, "Кения и Колумбия, люблю лёгкость и кислинку"

Quiz: `method=drip milk=no now=special taste=bright`. This person drinks 3–5 cups a day from a drip machine, grinds beans on an old Bork grinder, likes fruity and floral notes, tries new coffees all the time because of "интересные дескрипторы", and goes to other roasters "когда хочется чего то необычного".

## Step 1: quiz offer, Руанда Гицумби

**Offer.** Lead: «Начните с яркого кофе: вы уже пьёте спешелти и выбрали ягоды и цитрусы. Это начало, а не приговор…». Cards: «Начните с него» Руанда Гицумби (bergamot, raspberry, currant), «Мягче» Колумбия Антиокия, «Необычнее» Колумбия Вуш-Вуш. **Bought: Rwanda.** Currant and raspberry read like the Kenya they already love, and the card is the one the site recommends. Antioquia is "softer", which goes against what they want. Vush-Vush was tempting but felt like a second bag.

**Cup.** Washed Red Bourbon: acidity 4, body 3, sweetness 5. In a paper-filter drip machine, black, it makes a clean, juicy berry cup with a tea finish. Taps: **Кислинка: в самый раз** (bright and close to Kenya, not sour for them). **Плотность: в самый раз** (medium body through paper doesn't "load" the cup).

**Site's reply.** «Руанда Гицумби: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:». It offered «Снова», «Похожий» Кения Найваша ПБ («легче (плотность 2 против 3), ярче (кислотность 4,5 против 4)») and «По вашему вкусу» Вуш-Вуш. It listened. The Kenya card speaks this person's language exactly. One puzzle: «Пока это догадка по ответам подбора» shows even though they have just given real feedback.

**Verdict: fits.** A bright berry Rwanda is the right first bag for a Kenya and Colombia fan.

## Step 2: Кения Найваша ПБ

**Bought: Kenya Naivasha PB.** It is labelled «Похожий» and described as "легче, ярче", which is the person's own wording ("лёгкость и кислинка"). The only doubt is the card «use: Ручное заваривание»: their brewer is a machine, but they would ignore that.

**Cup.** Peaberry Kenya: acidity 4.5, body 2, blackberry, currant, black tea. In the drip machine this is their ideal cup. Taps: **Кислинка: в самый раз** and **Плотность: в самый раз**. This is "their" Kenya, with nothing to change.

**Site's reply.** «Кения Найваша ПБ: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:». The «Похожий» card is Колумбия Чайна Альта, which is «плотнее (плотность 3 против 2), мягче (кислотность 4 против 4,5)». That is a step back on both scales they care about. Vush-Vush was «По вашему вкусу» for the third time running. Internally the profile «moved: ярче и легче» after two "ok"s. The taste line still says «Пока это догадка по ответам подбора. После 1 ответа — назовём ваш вкус словами.»

**Verdict: fits.** The Kenya was a bullseye. The next "similar" card points away from what they just praised, but they still have «Снова» and a tempting third option.

## Step 3: Колумбия Вуш-Вуш

**Bought: Vush-Vush.** It had been on screen three times. It is Colombian, light and bright, and "барбариски, грушевый сидр" are exactly the "интересные дескрипторы" that make this person try something. Choosing it here saves a trip to another roaster for something unusual.

**Cup.** Natural process with 24 h oxidation and 3 days sealed: acidity 4, body 2, sweetness 5, fermentation 1. In the drip machine it is light, candy-sweet and fruity, with a gentle funk, nothing boozy. Taps: **Кислинка: в самый раз**. **Плотность: в самый раз** (light, which is what they like). **Необычность: в самый раз**. The light fermentation is fun for a daily cup, and they would still go elsewhere for something wild.

**Site's reply.** «Колумбия Вуш-Вуш: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:». The taste line now reads **«Яркий и лёгкий, без ферментации, уже довольно точно.»** The person just said a fermented coffee's unusualness was "в самый раз", so «без ферментации» contradicts their last answer, and «уже довольно точно» makes it worse. Offers: «Похожий» Колумбия Ла Сирена («Та же кислотность (4) и плотность (2)… необычнее (фермент 2 против 1)»), which is reasonable and they might well try it. «По вашему вкусу» is Чайна Альта, a body-3 coffee, under a line that calls their taste «лёгкий» after three answers that all picked body 2–3 as fine and Kenya (2) as the ideal. Internally «moved: мягче, легче и необычнее», while the text shown to them says "no fermentation".

**Verdict: questionable.** The cards are acceptable, but the summary contradicts what they said, and the «По вашему вкусу» pick is heavier than the taste the site has just named.

## Overall

The loop found this person a keeper in **two orders** (Kenya Naivasha PB), and Vush-Vush is a likely regular too. They would keep buying, mostly because the «Похожий» card in step 1 spoke their language (lighter, brighter). After that, three "в самый раз" answers taught the site very little, and its words started to drift from what it was told.

**Biggest problem:** the taste summary and the «По вашему вкусу» card contradict the answers. After a fermented coffee rated «в самый раз» on unusualness, the site says «без ферментации, уже довольно точно» and offers a denser coffee as "your taste". An attentive customer who reads descriptors closely (this one does) would distrust the "we know your taste" claim at the very moment it first appears. Smaller issues:
- «Пока это догадка по ответам подбора» stays on screen after real feedback.
- «Похожий» after an "ok" can move away on both scales (Chayna Alta after Kenya).
- The orders defaulted to «помол для фильтра» for someone who grinds their own beans. The quiz card said «или в зёрнах, если мелете сами», but the later cards and the orders dropped that option.

**Fix:** build the taste text and the «По вашему вкусу» pick from the coffees rated «в самый раз», not from a profile point that drifts. If any coffee with fermentation ≥ 1 was rated ok on unusualness, say «можно с лёгкой ферментацией». Keep «По вашему вкусу» inside the acidity and body range of the coffees rated ok (here acidity ≥ 4, body ≤ 2), so it never offers a heavier coffee than the person has confirmed.
