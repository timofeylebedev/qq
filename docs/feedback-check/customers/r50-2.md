# r50, round 2: daily espresso drinker at home who buys on a budget and distrusts new coffees

Profile in brief: a portafilter machine with a built-in grinder. He drinks espresso most days, sometimes a flat white or a raf with syrup. He likes a medium roast and blends. He buys 1 kg a month and is used to paying «до 2000₽». He distrusts new coffees («с недоверием отношусь к новому») but would take a barista's tip. He used to buy one of QQ's cheapest bags.

Quiz: method=espresso, milk=some, now=shop, taste=dunno. State file: `/home/claude/work/sim/state/r50-2.json`.

## Step 1
**Offered:** the same as round 1. Lead: «Вкус пока не знаем — поэтому три разных кофе…». The three cards:
- Q-Optimistic, «Мягкий и плотный»: «Почти без кислинки, очень плотный… Смесь с робустой»
- Эфиопия Бекеле, «Мягкий»
- Q-Finch, «Плотный»

There are still no prices.

**Bought:** Q-Optimistic. It is a familiar-sounding blend with no sourness and probably the cheapest of the three.

**Cup.** Thick, cocoa-like, a little bitter, and the shot is the kind he is used to. But the bag arrived «помол для эспрессо», and he grinds his own beans in his machine. A kilo of pre-ground coffee goes stale over a month.

**Taps:**
- First call: `issue=grind need=beans`.
- Second call: bright=ok, body=ok, milk=no, because the taste itself was right.

**Site's reply.**
- After the grind answer: «не сдвинулся: не про вкус», with the odd lead «Пока нет ответов про вкус: ответьте в заказе…» and only one card, Q-Punch. The card still said «помол для эспрессо».
- After the taste answer: «Q-Optimistic: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:». The cards were «Снова» Q-Optimistic, «По вашему вкусу» Q-Finch and «По вашему вкусу» Уганда Луна.

It listened on taste. On grind, the cards still read «помол для эспрессо» right after he asked for beans, so he would doubt the change had been saved. The lead promises «или похожий», but no card is labelled «Похожий». Both other cards have «кислинка».

**Verdict: fits.** The coffee he liked comes first, but the grind label makes him doubt it remembered.

## Step 2
**Bought:** Q-Optimistic again, «Снова». The order arrived **«В зернах»**, so the beans request did work. The card still says «помол для эспрессо», though.

**Cup.** The same coffee in beans from his own grinder, so it is fresher. This month he drank it mostly as flat whites and rafs. It is dense enough for milk and has no sourness.

**Taps:** bright=ok, body=ok, milk=yes.

**Site's reply.** The same lead and the same three cards, and the counter says «Ещё 1 ответ — и назовём ваш вкус словами». That is fine.

**Verdict: fits.** He has a bag he will keep buying, in the form he wants.

## Step 3
**Bought:** Q-Finch, «По вашему вкусу». After two good bags he was ready to try one tip. It is a blend, «плотный», with «молочный шоколад, орехи», and only «лёгкая кислинка».

**Cup.** Brazil 70%, Ethiopia (washed) 30%. Acidity 3/5, body 4/5, «лёгкой цитрусовой кислотностью». As his everyday straight espresso it tastes sourish and somewhat thinner than the robusta blend he is used to. «Лёгкая» undersold it for him.

**Taps:** bright=less (sour in the shot, his main complaint), body=ok (lighter, but not the problem), milk=no.

**Site's reply.** «Q-Finch (Финч бленд): вы сказали «слишком ярко». Следующий — мягче:». The cards:
- «Мягче»: Q-Punch, «Почти без кислинки… Для сравнения, Q-Finch: лёгкая кислинка»
- «Тоже мягче»: Уганда Луна, «Мягкая кислинка… цитрусы»
- «Снова»: Q-Optimistic, «Вы сказали о нём «в самый раз»…»

Taste line: «Мягкий и плотный, без ферментации, уже довольно точно».

This time it listened. Q-Punch is a sensible next step, the favourite is kept, and the taste summary agrees with the cards. One thing puzzles him: Уганда Луна is called «Тоже мягче» with «мягкая кислинка… цитрусы», but he can't tell whether «мягкая» means less than «лёгкая». It reads like the same sourness again. He would take Q-Optimistic, or maybe Q-Punch for his rafs.

**Verdict: fits.** It understood «too sour», kept his favourite and offered a fair low-acid blend. The Uganda card is the weak spot.

## Overall
The loop worked. He found a coffee he would keep buying on the first order (Q-Optimistic), in beans from the second order on, and the third answer moved him in the right direction without losing that favourite. He would stay with QQ instead of going back to Ozon, as long as the price is close to «до 2000₽», and the site still doesn't show it.

**Biggest problem:** the grind answer took effect (the order came «В зернах»), but every card he saw afterwards still says «помол для эспрессо». A distrustful customer reads that as «they ignored me». The reply to the grind answer itself was also an odd one-card offer: «Пока нет ответов про вкус».

**One concrete fix:** build each card's «set» line from the saved preference («обжарка под эспрессо, в зёрнах»), and after `issue=grind` confirm it in words, e.g. «Поняли: дальше присылаем в зёрнах». Also stop calling a citrus coffee «мягче» unless its acidity is clearly lower, and say so in plain words, e.g. «кислинки меньше, чем в Q-Finch».

## Against round 1
- **Better:** the coffee he rated «в самый раз» now stays on the screen as «Снова» after a negative answer. In round 1 it vanished, and that was the main failure. The taste line no longer contradicts the cards.
- **Better:** he can now ask for beans, and the order actually arrives in beans.
- **Worse:** the «Похожий» card is gone after an «ok» answer, though the lead still promises «или похожий». The other two cards both have «кислинка», so the safe second option for a cautious buyer has disappeared.
- **Still wrong:** the cards show no prices, the set line shows the wrong grind, and «мягкая кислинка» vs «лёгкая кислинка» is too vague for an ordinary drinker.
