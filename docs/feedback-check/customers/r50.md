# r50: daily espresso drinker at home who buys on a budget and distrusts new coffees

Profile in brief: a portafilter machine with a built-in grinder. He drinks espresso most days, sometimes a flat white or a raf with syrup, and now and then uses a pour-over. He likes a medium roast and blends. He buys 1 kg a month on marketplaces and is used to paying «до 2000₽»; he says he is unlikely to pay more and wouldn't notice the difference. On new coffees: «с недоверием отношусь к новому», though he would try one if a barista suggested it. He is a former QQ customer who used to take «почти самое недорогое».

Quiz: method=espresso, milk=some, now=shop, taste=dunno.

## Step 1
**Offered.** Lead: «Вкус пока не знаем — поэтому три разных кофе. Попробуйте и поймёте, какой ваш…». The three cards:
- Q-Optimistic, «Мягкий и плотный»: «Почти без кислинки, очень плотный… Смесь с робустой: крепче и с горчинкой»
- Эфиопия Бекеле, «Мягкий»
- Q-Finch, «Плотный»

Every card begins with «QQ советует его для капучино и латте». No prices were shown.

**Bought:** Q-Optimistic. It is a blend, it has almost no acidity, and it is the kind of «классика» he already drinks. A blend with robusta also reads as the cheapest bag, which is how he has always chosen. He would skip the Ethiopian: a single origin with «цветы» sounds both expensive and sour to him.

**Cup.** Brazil 60%, Colombia 20%, robusta 20%. Acidity 1/5, body 5/5. As a straight espresso it is thick and cocoa-like with a little bitterness, which is just what he is used to. This first bag he drank mostly as espresso.

**Taps:** bright=ok (no sourness, which is what he wants), body=ok (thick is what he expects from espresso), milk=no.

**Site's reply.** «Q-Optimistic: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:». The cards were «Снова» Q-Optimistic, «Похожий» Q-Punch and «По вашему вкусу» Q-Finch. It clearly listened, and «Снова» first is exactly right for someone who distrusts new things.

Two small issues:
- The bag arrived ground «помол для эспрессо», although he grinds his own beans. The quiz never asks about that, and the set line «или в зёрнах, если мелете сами» is easy to miss.
- «Пока это догадка по ответам подбора» is a little cryptic.

**Verdict: fits.** It offered a cheap-looking, familiar blend, and after the bag he liked it put that same bag first.

## Step 2
**Offered:** the same three cards as above. **Bought:** Q-Optimistic again. This is what he really does: if a bag is fine, he buys it again.

**Cup.** The same coffee, but this month he drank it more as flat whites and rafs with syrup. With milk the body still holds up, and there is no acidity to be dulled. It works well.

**Taps:** bright=ok, body=ok, milk=yes.

**Site's reply.** Exactly the same lead and the same three cards. The counter now says «После 1 ответа — назовём ваш вкус словами». That is honest, but nothing changed for him. That is acceptable, because nothing needed to change.

**Verdict: fits.** He has found a coffee he keeps buying, and the site doesn't push him away from it.

## Step 3
**Bought:** Q-Punch, the «Похожий» card. After two good bags he was willing to take one suggestion from the roaster. «Похожий» with the same «почти без кислинки, очень плотный» text feels like a barista's tip and doesn't feel risky.

**Cup.** Brazil 60%, robusta 40%, twice the robusta in Q-Optimistic. In a raf it would be great, since the roaster calls it «Классический бленд под молоко». As his everyday straight espresso, though, it is heavier and more bitter, closer to the marketplace coffee he wanted to get away from. The form has no «bitter» tap. The nearest thing he can find is «Слишком плотно».

**Taps:** bright=ok (still no sourness, which is fine), body=less (too heavy and harsh as straight espresso), milk=no.

**Site's reply.** «Q-Punch: вы сказали «слишком плотно». Следующий — легче:». The cards were:
- «Легче»: Эфиопия Бекеле, «Плотность 3 (Q-Punch — 5). Мягкая кислинка…»
- «Тоже легче»: Уганда Луна, «Мягкая кислинка… цитрусы»
- «По вашему вкусу»: Q-Finch

At the same time the taste line says «Мягкий и плотный, без ферментации, уже довольно точно.»

Problems from his side:
- **Q-Optimistic is gone.** He said «в самый раз» to it twice, and it was the obvious answer to «Q-Punch was too much». Now there is no «Снова» card at all. He would go back to his old bag and wouldn't need the site to do it.
- **The page contradicts itself.** It says his taste is «мягкий и плотный… довольно точно», then offers two medium-bodied single origins with «кислинка» and «цитрусы». To him «кислинка» means sour, and he has never asked for it.
- **Price is invisible.** Single origins probably cost more than blends, and that is the thing he cares about most.
- **He swings too far.** One «слишком плотно» about a 40%-robusta blend pushes him from body 5 to body 3. A 20%-robusta blend at body 4–5 would have been the real middle.

**Verdict: wrong.** It forgot the coffee he had confirmed twice. It also read «too heavy and bitter» as «wants lighter and brighter».

## Overall
The loop helped in step 1: on the first order he found a coffee he would keep buying, Q-Optimistic. Steps 1 and 2 behaved well. In step 3, one corrective tap on a «similar» bag wiped out his confirmed favourite and moved him towards acidic single origins. A cautious, price-driven customer like him would stop trusting the offers and go back to «просто беру одну из самых дешевых пачек», or to Ozon.

**Biggest problem:** a negative answer about the most recent bag overrides earlier positive answers. The coffee he said «в самый раз» to twice is no longer offered at all, and the summary of his taste («мягкий и плотный… довольно точно») disagrees with the cards shown under it.

**One concrete fix:** when the latest answer is «less» after a «Похожий» pick, and the customer has an earlier bag rated «в самый раз», put that bag first as «Снова: вам понравился Q-Optimistic». Choose the move between the two bags, for example by robusta share or body 4, rather than jumping two steps on body and adding acidity. A cheaper fix to do alongside it: show the price per kg on the cards, and ask in the quiz whether the person grinds their own beans.
