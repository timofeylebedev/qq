# r76: pour-over, light roast, floral/berry, former QQ customer who left

Quiz: method=pourover, milk=no, now=special, taste=wild. She grinds on a Comandante, drinks it black, and chases "яркие дескрипторы". She left QQ because "в последние пару раз не понравились зерна".

## Step 1
**Offered:** the lead was «Начните с необычного кофе: вы уже пьёте спешелти и хотите необычного. Это начало, а не приговор…». The three cards were Бразилия Ранчо Гранде («Начните с него», grapefruit/грильяж/fruit liqueur, «Лёгкая кислинка»), Колумбия Ла Сирена («Ярче», strawberry jam/citrus/black tea) and Кения Кисии («Необычнее»). The card texts run together with no punctuation: «необычный, как вы хотели Лёгкая кислинка…». Кисии's label «Необычнее первого: с ферментацией» is puzzling, because the first card already says «заметная ферментация» on the same scale. A Kenya with «Лёгкая кислинка» would also make her suspicious.
**Bought:** Ла Сирена. Strawberry jam plus "ярче" is exactly her brief. A Brazil with light acidity and грильяж is what she'd skip.
**Cup:** a co-fermented lot (yeast, glucose, 60° Brix), acidity 4, body 2, sweetness 4, ferment 2. The text has the producer and the process in detail, which is the "полное и понятное описание" she asked for. On her V60 it's a clean, juicy, tea-like strawberry cup.
**Taps:** bright=ok (bright is what she wants, and 4 is plenty), body=ok (a light tea body is right for a filter), unusual=ok (noticeably experimental without being boozy).
**Site said:** «Колумбия Ла Сирена: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:». The internal "moved" was «ярче и легче», although she asked for no change. The "Похожий" card was Вуш-Вуш: «Та же кислотность (4) и плотность (2)… Отличие: классичнее (фермент 1 против 2)». That listens on the first two rows but quietly takes a step down on unusualness. The 0–3 numbers mean nothing to her because she hasn't seen the scale. Ранчо Гранде came back as «По вашему вкусу».
**Verdict: fits.** The first pick was a hit and the reply acknowledged her "ok".

## Step 2
**Bought:** Вуш-Вуш. She "постоянно пробует", so she wouldn't take «Снова». A rare variety with барбариски and pear cider is the kind of novelty she buys for.
**Cup:** a natural with 3 days of anaerobic fermentation, acidity 4, body 2, sweetness 5, ferment 1. Through a pour-over that's sweet, juicy and fruity, and it reads clearly. It's very good.
**Taps:** bright=ok, body=ok, unusual=ok, all honest. It's a lovely cup.
**Site said:** the same template lead. "Moved" was «легче и классичнее», again after three "ok"s. "Похожий" was Кения Найваша ПБ: «классичнее (фермент 0 против 1), ярче (кислотность 4,5 против 4)». So a second "ok" on unusualness produced a second step toward classic, and the "similar" cards are walking her from ferment 2 to 1 to 0. The taste line «После 1 ответа — назовём ваш вкус словами» after two answers reads like a counter that moved its goalposts. It earlier said «После 2 ответов». Ранчо Гранде was again «По вашему вкусу».
**Verdict: questionable.** The offer is still tasty, but "similar" keeps drifting from what she said.

## Step 3
**Bought:** Найваша ПБ. Acidity 4.5, blackberry and currant are bright Kenyan berry notes, which she likes. To her it's more of a "базовый" washed lot than a QQ discovery, but she'd buy it.
**Cup:** washed peaberry, acidity 4.5, body 2, sweetness 5, ferment 0. On a V60 it's juicy and tart with blackcurrant and a tea finish. She'd enjoy it. With no fermentation there's no Необычность row, so she had no way to say "I miss the experimental stuff".
**Taps:** bright=ok, body=ok.
**Site said:** the taste line was «Яркий и лёгкий, с лёгкой ферментацией, уже довольно точно». "Bright and light" is right, but «с лёгкой ферментацией» understates her. She liked a ferment-2 lot and never asked for less. "Moved" was «ярче», again after "ok". "Похожий" was Руанда Гицумби: «плотнее (плотность 3 против 2), мягче (кислотность 4 против 4,5)». That moves both axes away from three "ok"s. Bergamot and raspberry might still tempt her. Ранчо Гранде showed up a fourth time as «По вашему вкусу» with «Лёгкая кислинка, средней плотности». That flatly contradicts the site's own «Яркий и лёгкий» one line above, and she'd stop trusting the label.
**Verdict: questionable.** "Again" is right, but "Похожий" and "По вашему вкусу" both drift from what she said.

## Overall
Did it help? Yes, at first: two of three orders (Ла Сирена, Вуш-Вуш) are coffees she'd rebuy, and she found the first in one order. But every "в самый раз" was followed by a "similar" one step further from her taste: ferment 2→1→0, then a denser, softer Rwanda. The stated taste profile was quietly lowered to «лёгкой ферментацией». Someone who left QQ because recent beans disappointed her would read this as the site steering her back toward ordinary coffee.

Biggest problem: the «Похожий» card always differs by at least one scale step, even when she answered "ok" on every row. The «По вашему вкусу» slot is stuck on Ранчо Гранде, which contradicts the site's own taste summary. Exposing numbers like «фермент 1 против 2» makes that drift visible but not understandable.

Fix: after an all-"ok" answer, make «Похожий» hold every scale she approved, including ferment, and vary only the descriptors (for example "the same brightness and experimental level, but floral instead of strawberry"). Recompute «По вашему вкусу» from the feedback so it never shows a coffee the summary line contradicts. Replace the raw 0–3 numbers with words («такой же экспериментальный», «чуть классичнее»).
