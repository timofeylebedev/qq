## Step 1: Уганда Луна

**Offer.** Same as round 1. Lead: «Начните с мягкого кофе: вы пьёте с молоком, привыкли к кофе из магазина и выбрали карамель и фрукты…» Cards: «Начните с него» Уганда Луна, «Ярче» Вьетнам Куа Мон, «Тоже подходит» Эфиопия Бекеле. All three are set to «Обжарка под фильтр, помол для фильтра — или в зёрнах, если мелете сами». There is still no drip-bag option. I bought **Уганда Луна** for the same reasons as before: it is the first pick, «QQ советует его для капучино и латте» answers her milk question, and «молочный шоколад» sounds safe. The «Ярче» card still says «Лёгкая кислинка», which to her reads as milder than «Мягкая».

**Cup.** It arrived as `grind: Фильтр`, loose ground coffee, which she can't brew the drip-bag way. I tapped **issue=grind need=bags**. She improvised by steeping it in a mug and straining it (sent as method=press), with milk. It was soft, sweet (sweetness 5), not sour (acidity 2), and medium-bodied. Taps: **bright=ok, body=ok**.

**Site's answer.** After the grind answer, the engine note was «не сдвинулся: не про вкус». That is clearer than round 1, but the site showed **no picks at all**, just «Пока нет ответов про вкус: ответьте в заказе…». Nothing acknowledged drip bags. After the taste answer: «Уганда Луна: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:» with «Снова» Уганда, «Похожий» Бекеле («Как у него: кислинка и плотность…») and «По вашему вкусу» Q-Finch. All three were again **«в зёрнах»**, right after she asked for bags. Q-Finch is «обжарка под эспрессо», which is wrong for her. The engine note «уточнился» replaces round 1's odd «мягче и легче».

**Verdict: questionable.** The taste pick was right, but her «need=bags» was ignored and the format got worse.

## Overall

Taste-wise, the loop found her coffee in order 1, the same as in round 1. Уганда Луна is soft, sweet, works with milk and isn't sour, and she'd keep buying it. Format-wise it still fails. The new `need=` answer changes nothing she can see: not the card text, not the delivered grind, not even a line saying «запомнили». After order 1 every bag arrived as whole beans, which she can't use. She would most likely leave after order 2, and she'd feel unheard rather than just badly served, because this time the site asked her what she needs and then ignored the answer.

**Biggest problem:** the `need=bags|ground` answer is collected but not applied. The «set» line stays «в зёрнах», and orders 2 and 3 arrived as whole beans.

**Fix:** after `need=…`, rewrite the «set» line on every later card and the default form of the next order to match: «дрип-пакеты» if QQ sells that lot in bags, otherwise «помол для фильтра» plus a line like «дрип-пакетов этого кофе нет — пришлём молотым». Confirm it right away («Запомнили: дрип-пакеты») instead of showing no picks.

## Against round 1

- **Better:** the engine notes are clearer («не сдвинулся: не про вкус», «уточнился» instead of «мягче и легче» after «в самый раз»), and the counter «Ещё N ответа — и назовём ваш вкус словами» is plain.
- **Worse:** the reply to a grind answer now shows no picks at all, and ignoring an explicit «need=bags» feels worse than ignoring a bare complaint.
- **Still wrong:** the switch from ground to «в зёрнах» after a grind complaint; no drip-bag option anywhere; espresso-roast picks for a pourover drinker; «Лёгкая кислинка» on the «Ярче» card; «урбеч».
