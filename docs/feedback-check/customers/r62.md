# r62: drip-bag drinker, with milk, no grinder, wary of new coffee

Quiz: `method=pourover milk=yes now=shop taste=caramel`. Profile in short: drinks drip bags («простой и быстрый» process), always with milk, has no grinder, likes chocolate/nut/berry notes, calls her own taste «обывательский», distrusts new coffee, and her biggest worry is «кислый/горький или нет… сочетается ли дрип с молоком».

## Step 1: Уганда Луна

**Offer.** Lead: «Начните с мягкого кофе: вы пьёте с молоком, привыкли к кофе из магазина и выбрали карамель и фрукты… на странице кофе сразу будут ваши обжарка и помол.» Cards: «Начните с него» Уганда Луна, «Ярче» Вьетнам Куа Мон, «Тоже подходит» Эфиопия Бекеле. All three are set to «Обжарка под фильтр, помол для фильтра — или в зёрнах, если мелете сами». I bought **Уганда Луна** because it is the first pick, «QQ советует его для капучино и латте» answers her milk question, and «молочный шоколад» sounds safe. Small puzzle: the «Ярче» card says «Лёгкая кислинка», and the safe one says «Мягкая кислинка». To her, «лёгкая» sounds milder, not brighter.

**Cup.** The bag arrived as `grind: Фильтр`, i.e. loose ground coffee and not drip bags. She has no dripper, only a habit of drip bags, so she can't brew it the way she normally does. I tapped **issue=grind**. She then improvised: she steeped it in a mug and strained it (sent as method=press), with milk. Acidity 2, sweetness 5, body 3, so with milk it is a soft, sweet, chocolatey cup, nothing sour. Taps: **bright=ok** (no sourness, which is what she fears), **body=ok** (about as full as her usual drip bag with milk).

**Site's answer.** After the grind answer, the lead was «Пока нет ответов про вкус…», and all three picks were still «помол для фильтра», so it said nothing about the grind. After the taste answer: «Уганда Луна: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:» with «Снова» Уганда, «Похожий» Бекеле («Та же кислотность (2) и плотность (3)…») and «По вашему вкусу» Q-Finch. The taste part shows it listened. But every pick has now silently switched to **«в зёрнах»**, after she reported a grind problem and when she has no grinder. Q-Finch is also «обжарка под эспрессо», which is wrong for a drip-bag person. The engine also reported «мягче и легче» after two «в самый раз» taps.

**Verdict: questionable.** The taste pick was right, but the coffee came in a form she can't use, and her complaint about it made things worse.

## Step 2: Уганда Луна again

**Offer.** Same as the end of step 1: «Снова» Уганда («В самый раз — так вы сказали в прошлый раз.»), Бекеле, Q-Finch, all «в зёрнах». She bought **Уганда Луна** again. She liked it and doesn't trust new coffee. There was no way to pick ground coffee or drip bags.

**Cup.** It arrived as `grind: В зернах`, whole beans she cannot grind. **issue=grind** again. She had a coffee shop grind it for her, then steeped it in a mug with milk as before: same soft, sweet, chocolatey cup. **bright=ok, body=ok.**

**Site's answer.** The grind answer: no change, still «в зёрнах». The taste answer: the same «Снова / Похожий» lead. Q-Finch was swapped for «Бразилия Санта Мария» («молочный шоколад, урбеч, яблоко», «обжарка под эспрессо, в зёрнах»). She wouldn't know «урбеч», and it is an espresso roast again. The engine said «легче» after «в самый раз».

**Verdict: wrong.** Two grind complaints were ignored, and she paid twice for beans she had to take to a coffee shop.

## Step 3: Уганда Луна, third time

**Offer.** Unchanged: Уганда / Бекеле / Санта Мария, all «в зёрнах». She bought **Уганда Луна** for the third time. Taste-wise it is the coffee she'd keep. Бекеле's card would tempt her a little, but it brings nothing new she can see except «цветы».

**Cup.** Beans again. **issue=grind**, then **bright=ok, body=ok** after another trip to the coffee shop to grind it.

**Final offer.** «Мягкий, без ферментации, уже довольно точно.» That is a correct and plain summary of her taste, and she'd understand it. The picks are the same three, all still «в зёрнах».

**Verdict: questionable.** It got her taste right and in plain words, but three grind complaints in a row changed nothing, and the format got worse after the first one.

## Overall

On taste, the loop worked from order 1. Уганда Луна is soft, sweet, works with milk, isn't sour, and is the coffee she would keep buying. The site's «Снова» and «Похожий» picks and the final taste line are sensible. On format it failed her. She lives on drip bags, the site never offered drip bags, the first bag was loose ground coffee, and after she said «помол не подошёл» every later offer and delivery switched to whole beans. She would most likely stop buying after order 2. She already distrusts online coffee, and being told «в зёрнах, если мелете сами» when she has said she doesn't grind would confirm that.

**Biggest problem:** `issue=grind` has no visible effect except a bad one. The setting flips from «помол для фильтра» to «в зёрнах», the opposite of what someone without a grinder needs. And «pourover» covers both a dripper and drip bags, so she was never asked which one she uses.

**Fix:** when someone answers «помол не подошёл», ask one follow-up question with these options: «у меня дрип-пакеты / нет кофемолки / мелю сам / другой способ». Save the answer as the default form for every later card and order (drip bags if QQ has them, otherwise ground for that method), and never fall back to «в зёрнах» unless the person said they grind. Also split «воронка» and «дрип-пакет» in the quiz.

Minor: the engine reported «мягче и легче» / «легче» after all-«в самый раз» answers; «Лёгкая кислинка» on the «Ярче» card reads as milder than «Мягкая»; espresso-roast picks were shown to a pourover drinker; «урбеч» is jargon for this customer.
