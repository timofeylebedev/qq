Quiz: `method=drip milk=no now=special taste=bright`. The person and their honest answers are the same as in round 1. This person grinds their own beans on an old Bork grinder, so a bag that arrives ground is a problem for them.

**Offer.** The quiz screen was unchanged from round 1. Lead: «Начните с яркого кофе: вы уже пьёте спешелти и выбрали ягоды и цитрусы…». Cards: «Начните с него» Руанда Гицумби, «Мягче» Колумбия Антиокия, «Необычнее» Колумбия Вуш-Вуш. - First call, **issue=grind, need=beans**: "I grind myself, send beans."
- Second call, **Кислинка: в самый раз, Плотность: в самый раз**, as in round 1.

**Site's reply.** To the grind answer the site said «Пока нет ответов про вкус: ответьте в заказе, и здесь появится кофе под вас.» and showed three «По вашему вкусу» cards, all still «помол для фильтра». The beans request got no acknowledgement on screen. To the taste answer it said «Руанда Гицумби: вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:», with «Похожий» Кения Найваша ПБ: «Кислинка ярче и легче.» The pick is right. The wording is clumsy: acidity is "brighter", but it is the coffee, not the acidity, that is "lighter". Round 1's numbers («плотность 2 против 3») are gone.

**Verdict: fits.** The coffee is right. The grind answer looks ignored on screen, but see Step 2.

## Step 3: Колумбия Вуш-Вуш

**Bought: Vush-Vush.** It is Colombian, light and bright, and «барбариски, грушевый сидр» is the kind of descriptor that makes this person try something. It arrived **in beans**.

**Cup.** Acidity 4, body 2, fermentation 1, natural process with a sealed ferment. In the drip machine it is light and candy-sweet with a gentle funk. Taps: **Кислинка: в самый раз, Плотность: в самый раз, Необычность: в самый раз.**

**Site's reply.** The taste line is now **«Яркий и лёгкий, можно с лёгкой ферментацией, уже довольно точно.»** This is accurate and in the person's own terms, where round 1 said «без ферментации». Offers:
- «Снова» Vush-Vush.
- «Похожий» Колумбия Ла Сирена: «Как у него: кислинка и плотность, необычнее» (strawberry jam). This is a reasonable next adventure; they would probably try it next, or alternate it with Kenya.
- «Снова» Kenya.

Every card still reads «помол для фильтра» after two orders delivered in beans.

**Verdict: fits.** The summary matches what they said, and every card is something they would buy.

## Overall

The loop found a keeper in **two orders** (Kenya), and by order three it described their taste correctly and offered only coffees in their range: two past favourites and one slightly more unusual step. Its picks no longer drift away after "в самый раз".

**Biggest problem:** the grind request works but is invisible. After `issue=grind need=beans`, the reply talks only about taste, and every later card still says «помол для фильтра», even though orders 2 and 3 arrived «В зернах». Someone who grinds their own would not trust the card and might write to support or leave a note on the order.

**Fix:** after a grind answer, confirm it in one line («Дальше пришлём в зёрнах»), and print the saved choice on every card («обжарка под фильтр, в зёрнах»). Also put the numbers back in the comparison lines, or write them as short clauses («та же плотность, кислинка мягче, необычнее»). The current «Как у него: плотность, кислинка мягче и необычнее» is hard to parse.

## Against round 1

- **Better:** the taste summary now matches the answers («можно с лёгкой ферментацией» instead of «без ферментации»). The denser «По вашему вкусу» pick (Чайна Альта) has left the later offers. The progress line now counts answers given after delivery.
- **Better:** the beans request is new, and it really changes what is delivered (orders 2 and 3 came in beans).
- **Worse:** the comparison texts lost their numbers and became garbled («Кислинка ярче и легче», «Как у него: плотность, кислинка мягче и необычнее»).
- **Still wrong:** the cards ignore the saved grind («помол для фильтра»). The reply to a grind complaint says nothing about grind. The quiz still never asks whether the person grinds their own, so the first bag arrives ground.
