## Step 3: Q-Finch

**Bought** Q-Finch.
**Cup.** Acidity 3, body 4, sweetness 4. The roaster calls it "Сочный, чистый, сбалансированный… легкой цитрусовой кислотностью". Under lots of milk and sugar it gives chocolate, nuts and a hint of dried berry. It's a little gentler than Q-Profit, but just as full. **Taps:** «В самый раз» on both rows.
**Reply.** "Q-Finch (Финч бленд): вы сказали «в самый раз». Возьмите его снова или похожий:" The taste line read "Плотный, без ферментации, уже довольно точно." Cards: "Снова" Q-Finch; "Похожий" Q-Berry ("Кислинка и плотность те же. Во вкусе сухофрукты, чай, шоколад, ягоды."); "Снова" Q-Profit ("Вы сказали о нём «в самый раз»"). All three are dense, non-fermented blends for milk, two of them proven. Q-Berry is her "шоколад + ягоды" exactly, and she'd take it. Two cards labelled "Снова" side by side are slightly confusing, but the card texts make them clear.
**Verdict: fits.** Every card matches the taste line and her answers.

## Overall

**The loop works for her.** She found a keeper (Q-Profit) in two orders and a second one (Q-Finch) in three. She ended with a choice of three dense chocolate-and-berry blends, which suits someone who "постоянно пробую новое" but wants it "насыщенный". She still never learns what «кислинка» means for her.

**Biggest problem:** early on, the «По вашему вкусу» card is still Уганда Луна, which has medium body. It appeared at both step 1 and step 2, although she never asked for a lighter coffee. Under lots of milk it is exactly the thin cup she complains about.

**Fix:** for milk drinkers who haven't asked for «легче», the "fit" card should keep the body of the coffee she just rated (body 4 or more here). Words like «ферментация» need a gloss on the card, for example "винный, необычный вкус".
