## New problems

- **The fallback guards the asked row but not the others.** The header now says cards keep "within about one step on a row not asked about". That holds for move cards but not for the «Вот что ближе всего» cards:
  - Кисии's fallbacks drop F3 → 0 (6 cards);
  - Воносалам and ИПА lose their ferment the same way;
  - Ла Сирена loses its ferment, or jumps D2 → 4;
  - Пальма Реаль and Найваша swap A2 for A4.5 and back;
  - classic blends get the Excelsa (F0 → 3).

  The move highs of round 1 have partly moved here, behind an honest lead. *Fix:* apply the same one-step limit on unasked rows to fallback cards, and show fewer cards rather than break a row.
- **The «совсем другие по плотности или ферментации» reason is generated even when the difference is acidity** (Гицумби, Ранчо Гранде). The same pair is judged inconsistently: Гицумби's denser options are "совсем другие", while Чайна Альта, with the same codes, gets Q-Berry as «Плотнее».
- **A new label, «Ближе всего — он сам»**, offers the same coffee after «хочу ярче». Either add it to the agreed labels or show the lead alone.
