# The feedback loop's outside check

Session 6 of choosing coffee (26 Nov 2026). Two checks:
1. **Simulated customers:** six real respondents of the B2C survey, each played over three orders.
2. **A separate agent** checking, against the roasters' own texts, that the moves make sense.

Both were run on the build's command line (`build/feedback-sim.mjs`). Neither kind of agent saw the loop's rules.

## 1. Six survey respondents, three orders each

**Who:** six respondents of the B2C survey (`docs/quiz-check/respondents.json` has their quiz answers, coded blind in
session 4), chosen to spread over the map and over the ways people drink:

| id | how they drink (their words, short) | quiz answers |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | капельная кофеварка, светлая, «люблю лёгкость и кислинку», Кения и Колумбия | drip · no · special · bright |
| 50 | рожковая, эспрессо/флэт/раф, «одну из самых дешевых пачек», недоверие к новому | espresso · some · shop · dunno |
| 62 | дрип-пакеты с молоком, кофемолки нет, «перу точно не нравится» | pourover · yes · shop · caramel |
| 70 | автомат, много молока и сахар, тёмная, любила смесь «Сухой закон» | auto · yes · special · choc |
| 75 | турка с молоком и сиропом, «Колумбийские», «неплохо знаю процессы» | turka · yes · shop · caramel |
| 76 | воронка, Comandante, «необычными, яркими дескрипторами»; ушла от QQ | pourover · no · special · wild |

**How** (`brief.md`):
- One agent per customer was given the person's own survey answers. The verbatim answers are kept outside the
  repository; the names were removed.
- Each agent:
  - took the quiz with session 4's coded answers;
  - bought what the person would from what the site offered;
  - read the roaster's text and scales of the bag (`cup`) as what was in the cup, imagined through the person's brewing;
  - answered as the person would;
  - judged every step: fits / questionable / wrong.
- The agents never saw the rules. They saw only what the site shows.
- Three rounds; the rules were changed between rounds for what they found. Reports: `customers/r<id>.md` (round 1),
  `r<id>-2.md`, `r<id>-3.md`. Their state files: `states/`.

**Verdicts, 18 steps per round:**

| Round | fits | questionable | wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | 6 | 2 |
| 2 | 12 | 5 | 1 |
| 3 | 15 | 3 | 0 |

**What each round changed:**
- **Round 1:**
  - **50 (wrong):** one «слишком плотно» about a «Похожий» bag hid the coffee he had called «в самый раз» twice.
    → Coffees called «в самый раз» come back as «Снова».
  - **62 (wrong):** drip bags came as whole beans after «помол не подошёл». → «Как вам удобнее?» (beans / ground for my
    way / drip bags), kept and used for the next cards' grind; the reply confirms it.
  - **75:** «хочу ярче» on a 3, then «слишком ярко» on a 4.5, swung back to 3. → A move lands between earlier answers first.
  - **76:** «Похожий» drifted a step each time (ferment 2 → 1 → 0) → it keeps the scales approved.
  - **4, 76:** «без ферментации» after a fermented coffee was «в самый раз» → «можно с лёгкой / заметной ферментацией».
  - **All:** «Ваш вкус сдвинулся: плотнее» after «в самый раз» read as not listening → «уточнился». Numbers like
    «фермент 1 против 2» meant nothing → words.
- **Round 2:**
  - «Похожий» fell back to coffees that weren't similar → no fallback; the lead says when there is none.
  - The «Похожий» sentence was broken Russian → «Кислинка и плотность те же, ферментации больше.»
  - «По вашему вкусу» could still go against the last answer → it must go the asked way and stay inside the region.
  - Ferment became a soft wish there.
- **Round 3:**
  - «Тоже ярче» onto a dense fermented lot after «хочу ярче» alone → a move changes only what was asked.
  - A robusta blend offered to an arabica-only fruit lover → no robusta until the visitor has had one.

**Still questionable in round 3:**
- **50:** Q-Punch («Похожий», same scales as Q-Optimistic) was too bitter as espresso. There is no bitterness row, so he
  said «слишком плотно» (question `feedback-bitter`).
- **76, steps 2–3:** after two «в самый раз» she was offered her two coffees again and nothing new. Nothing new in QQ's
  range is that close (bright, light, fermented). This is a hole in the range, not a rule.

**The paths on the map:** `paths.json` (generated by `build/feedback-paths.mjs` from `states/*-3.json`) replays each
round-3 customer's purchases and answers on the final rules. The page: https://claude.ai/artifact/5WMhvZhhqrq9v4UGq7KeSH (source outside the repo; it reads `paths.json`).

**Limits:**
- The agents imagine the cup from the roaster's text; no one drank anything.
- The six are current or former QQ buyers, not newcomers.
- An agent knew its earlier rounds (each round was played from scratch, but it could compare).

## 2. A separate agent against the roasters' texts

**What it checked:**
- every card on the six paths;
- every row of `docs/feedback-output.md` that isn't «в самый раз»: the first move, each «у нас нет» sentence, the cards
  under it;
- the «Похожий» rows.

The truth was each coffee's own text in `src/data/products.json` (`about`, `short`, descriptors) and QQ's coded scales.
Where they disagree, it says which it went by.

| Pass | checked | flagged | high | medium | low |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (`moves.md`) | 254 | 102 | 21 | 53 | 28 |
| 2 (`moves-2.md`) | 256 | 82 | 10 | 35 | 37 |
| 3 (`moves-3.md`, the remaining items only) | — | 38 | **0** | 37 | 1 |

**What it changed:**
- **Pass 1:**
  - fermented coffees lost their ferment on a move about acidity or body (16 of 25) → a row not asked about keeps within
    about one step; ferment never more than 1.3 tamer;
  - «Плотнее» onto Q-Punch from bright lots → the same guard for every move;
  - the cards under «у нас нет» went back against the answer (30 of 51) → never against, fewer cards instead.
  - The build now checks all three on QQ's coded scales.
- **Pass 2:**
  - fallback cards still dropped the ferment of Кисии and Воносалам → every card keeps the rows not asked about;
  - «совсем другие по плотности» when the difference was acidity → the lead names the row that differs.
- **Pass 3:** «Ближе всего — он сам» re-offered the coffee just turned away from → at QQ's very corner the lead alone.

**What remains** (medium, `moves-3.md`):
- **Mostly QQ's coded scales against their own texts.** The equal-code trio Санта Мария / Антиокия / Мерури is «Похожий»
  to each other though the texts describe opposites. Кисии is coded 3/3/3 where its text reads acidity 4, body 4,
  ferment 2. Гуджи and Антиокия are coded 3 where their texts say bright. Бекеле, Ранчо Гранде and Куа Мон are coded too
  light, Чайна Альта too dense. Q-Punch's robusta origin. These are added to the questions for the roaster; the model
  follows the codes until Notion changes.
- **One rule:** «Снова» Q-Optimistic after «слишком плотно» on Q-Punch (equal body). Kept on purpose: the customer liked
  having his favourite back, and the label says he called it «в самый раз».

**Holes in the range:** 14 single answers of 111 (in `docs/feedback-output.md`) end with the lead alone. Nothing in
stock goes that way while keeping the other rows. They are mostly the fermented corner: Кисии, Воносалам, ИПА, Ла Сирена
and Ранчо Гранде, plus the lightest and brightest lots. That tells QQ where its range is thin.
